# Step 24 — reconciling payments with the provider

R3 stands: the statement is fetched by the one part of the code that talks
to the payment providers. R9 and the rest of the books stand: the
comparison is a report.

- Tomek gives a period: from a moment up to, but not including, another.
  The system fetches the statement of the provider that is in use
  (`PAYMENT_PROVIDER`) for that period and compares it with its own
  records of the same period: the payments that succeeded and the refunds
  of returns.
- **HD-126:** the period is two moments in time; the first must be before
  the second. A period that cannot be read, or that ends before it begins,
  is refused.
- Charges are matched by the charge id the provider gave. Three kinds of
  difference:
  - the provider has a charge the shop does not know (the case Tomek
    found: the provider charged the card, the answer never reached the
    shop, HD-05);
  - the shop has a successful payment the provider does not list;
  - both have it, with different amounts.
- A declined attempt is not money: it is on no statement and it is not a
  difference.
- Every difference says whether it is about a charge or a refund, which of
  the three kinds it is, the provider's charge id, the amount on the
  shop's side and the amount on the provider's side (none where a side
  does not have it), and the order.
- **HD-127:** for a charge the shop does not know, the order is found
  through the reference on the statement, which is the order's id (HD-11):
  when it names one of our orders, the difference shows that order;
  otherwise it shows none.
- **HD-128, refunds:** refunds are compared per charge: what was given back
  against a charge in the period according to the shop, and according to
  the provider. Only the provider has refunds for the charge: unknown to
  the shop. Only the shop has: unknown to the provider. Both, with
  different sums: the amounts differ. The difference carries the id of the
  charge the refunds are against.
- **HD-129:** the report also shows four sums for the period: charged and
  refunded according to the shop, charged and refunded according to the
  provider.
- **HD-130:** the comparison changes nothing: no order becomes paid, no
  payment or refund is added, corrected or removed, on either side. Tomek
  sorts each difference out by hand. (An order whose charge the shop does
  not know stays as it is, waiting or expired.)
- **HD-131:** when the provider cannot be reached or answers with an error,
  the caller is told the provider is unavailable, as for payments (HD-05).
  It is not reported as a list of differences.
- **HD-132:** what counts for the period is, for the shop, the moment the
  payment or refund was recorded, and for the provider, the time on the
  statement. A payment outside the period is not looked at, whatever the
  provider says about it.

## Not decided

Fixing a difference from the report (marking an order as paid, refunding a
charge nobody ordered), fees, payouts to the bank account, comparing both
providers at once.
