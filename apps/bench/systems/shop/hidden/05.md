# Step 5 — returns

- Only a shipped order takes returns, also one that already had a partial
  return.
- **HD-26b:** per product, never more returned in total than was ordered. A
  product that is not on the order cannot be returned. A return has at least
  one line, each at least 1.
- **HD-30:** the money back is the returned quantity times the price fixed
  on the order, not today's price.
- The refund goes against the charge of the order's successful payment, one
  refund per return.
- **HD-27:** each returned line is a stock movement: goods come in, marked
  as a return, with the order's id, in the warehouse the goods came back to
  (`MAIN` unless another is named).
- **HD-28:** when the provider fails, nothing happens: no return on record,
  no stock back, and the caller is told the provider is unavailable.
- **HD-29:** when everything on the order has been returned the order
  becomes "returned", a new final state; until then it stays shipped.
- Returns of an order can be listed, oldest first, each with its lines and
  the amount refunded.
