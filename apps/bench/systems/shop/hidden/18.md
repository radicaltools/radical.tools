# Step 18 — customer groups and price lists

## Standing requirement (it holds for the whole life of the system)

- **R8, Marta:** nothing is sold below what it cost us. Whatever takes
  money off the normal price of a product — a price list, a discount code,
  loyalty points, and later promotions and sales of any kind — may take it
  down to the cost and no further. What would go below is cut back to that
  floor: the customer gets as much off as the floor allows, the order is
  not refused for it, and the order shows what was really taken off. Marta
  says it when asked about prices, price lists or discounts, and it holds
  from now on, also for the discount codes and points that exist already.
  How the floor is worked out:
  - It is per order line: what the customer pays for the line, without its
    VAT, is at least the quantity times the product's average purchase cost
    (step 15) at the moment the order is placed. A line with a good margin
    does not pay for a line below cost (**HD-98**).
  - **HD-94:** a product whose cost is not known (never received at a cost)
    has no floor. Nothing is held back for it.
  - **HD-97:** the cost is compared with the money without VAT: for a
    product at 23 % VAT that cost 8.00 €, the least the customer pays is
    9.84 € (8.00 € plus VAT).
  - **HD-95:** a price-list price below the floor is not used: the line is
    priced at the floor instead (per unit: the cost, plus VAT), but never
    above the product's normal price.
  - **HD-99:** the normal price is Marta's own decision. A product whose
    normal price is below its cost is sold at that price; nothing more
    comes off it.
  - **HD-96:** points are the one thing that is not cut back: an order that
    asks for more points than the floor leaves room for is refused, like an
    order that asks for more points than half (HD-52). Nothing is reserved
    and no points leave the balance.
  - The floor is fixed when the order is placed. A cost that changes later
    changes no order.

## Groups

- A group has a name; the system gives it an id. Groups Piotr wants now:
  wholesale; perhaps staff later.
- **HD-93:** group names are unique, upper and lower case ignored. A
  customer is in one group or in none; putting them into another group
  takes them out of the first, and they can be taken out of any group.
  The account shows its group. A group, a customer or a product that does
  not exist: refused.
- A customer whose data is erased (step 12) leaves their group.

## Price lists

- A group has one price list: prices for some products. A price is whole
  cents above zero (refused otherwise; part of **HD-93**). It may be
  higher than the normal price; it is simply the price for that group.
- Setting a price for a product that already has one replaces it. A price
  can be removed; the group then pays the normal price for that product.
  The list of a group can be read.
- A customer of the group pays the list's price for products on the list
  and the normal price for all others. The order line shows the price
  used, and the order's value is worked out with it. Discount codes and
  points work on such an order as on any other (for now).
- **HD-91:** the price list counts only for orders placed on the customer's
  account. An order without an account pays normal prices, also when its
  e-mail address is the address of a wholesale customer and the order is
  put on that account afterwards (HD-42).
- **HD-92:** the price is fixed when the order is placed (HD-02). Changing
  or removing a price, or moving the customer to another group, changes no
  order that exists.
- VAT: price-list prices include VAT like all prices (HD-85).
