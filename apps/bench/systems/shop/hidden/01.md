# Step 1 — cancelling

- **HD-14:** only an order still waiting for payment can be cancelled. A
  paid or shipped one cannot: there is no way to give money back yet.
- **HD-15:** cancelling an order that is already cancelled is fine, not an
  error; nothing changes.
- Cancelling frees the reserved stock. It is not a stock movement: nothing
  left or entered the warehouse.
- A cancelled order cannot be paid or shipped.
