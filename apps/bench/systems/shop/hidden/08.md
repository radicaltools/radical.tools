# Step 8 — selling what is not on the shelf

R2 stands, and Marta will not give it up: "available" below zero or more
reserved than is on the shelf is exactly the spreadsheet mess she wants to
leave behind. Piotr wants the order taken; he does not care how the missing
units are counted. If asked: units that are not on the shelf are not
reserved, they are **backordered**.

- An order for more than is available is accepted. What is available is
  reserved as before; the rest of each line is backordered.
- **HD-38:** the stock figures of a product show how many units are
  backordered, and each order line shows how many of its units are
  backordered (zero when fully reserved).
- **HD-39:** when stock becomes available (a receipt, an adjustment up, a
  return, a cancelled or expired order) backordered lines are reserved from
  it, oldest order first, in the warehouse where the stock appeared.
- **HD-37:** a backordered order can be paid. It can be shipped only when
  nothing on it is backordered.
- Cancelling or expiring an order drops its backordered units.
