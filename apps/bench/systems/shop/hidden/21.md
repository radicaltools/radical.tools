# Step 21 — invoices

## Standing requirement (it holds for the whole life of the system)

- **R9, Tomek:** an invoice that has been issued is never changed and never
  deleted. Not its number, its date, its lines, its amounts or its VAT —
  whatever happens afterwards to the order, the product, its price, its
  category, the VAT rate or the customer's account. There is no way to edit
  an invoice, for anyone. Invoice numbers run per calendar year: the first
  invoice of a year is number 1, the next one 2, and so on, with no gap and
  no number used twice; something that fails or is refused uses up no
  number. When an invoice turns out to be wrong it is corrected by a credit
  note (and, if needed, a new invoice), never by touching it. The same
  holds for credit notes once they exist. The one thing that may go from an
  invoice is the buyer's personal data when the customer asks to be erased
  (R4): the buyer's name, e-mail address and postal address are taken off;
  the number, the date, the lines and the amounts stay. Tomek must be able
  to list all invoices of a year, in the order of their numbers.

## When and what

- The invoice is made by the system when the order is shipped, in the same
  step: no shipment without an invoice, no invoice without a shipment. An
  order that was never shipped (waiting, paid, cancelled, expired) has no
  invoice.
- One invoice per shipment, for the goods in that shipment. Today an order
  goes out whole, so an order has one invoice; if orders ever go out in
  parts, each shipment gets its own invoice for what is in it, and the
  invoices of an order add up to the order.
- The invoice shows: its number, its date, the order it is for, the buyer,
  its lines, its total and the VAT in it. A line shows the product (SKU and
  the product's name), the quantity, the unit price, the VAT rate, the
  amount of the line and the VAT in it.
- The amounts are what the customer paid: the lines' amounts after
  everything that was taken off (promotion, code, points), split over the
  lines as for VAT (HD-89). The lines add up to the invoice's total, their
  VAT adds up to its VAT (R7), and the total is the order's total. The
  amount without VAT is the total minus the VAT.
- **HD-114:** the invoice keeps its own copy of everything on it, as it was
  when it was issued: the product's name, the prices, the rates, the buyer.
  Renaming a product later, or changing the customer's account, changes no
  invoice (R9).
- **HD-115, the buyer:** the name on the customer's account at that moment
  (none for an order without an account), the order's e-mail address and
  the order's shipping address (none when the order has none).
- Orders without an account get an invoice like any other.

## Numbers and dates

- **HD-112:** the number is written as the year, a slash and the running
  number with at least four digits: `2026/0001`, `2026/0002`, …,
  `2026/10000`.
- **HD-113:** the date of the invoice is the day of the shipment: the day
  of the time the shipping call is told to assume, or today when it is
  told nothing. The year of the number is the year of that date. Any valid
  time is accepted, past or future; the system does not check it against
  the clock. A time that cannot be read is refused, and then nothing is
  shipped. Days are days in UTC (the servers run on UTC; Tomek accepts
  that for now).
- A shipment dated in another year takes the next number of that year,
  whenever it is entered.
- A shipment that is refused (order not paid, goods still on backorder)
  makes no invoice and uses no number.

## Erasing a customer (R4)

- When a customer is erased, the buyer's name, e-mail address and postal
  address on their invoices become empty (null). Everything else on the
  invoices stays, and the invoices stay in the lists.

## Not decided

The company's own details on the invoice, the buyer's VAT number, payment
terms, a PDF, sending the invoice by e-mail, invoices in other languages.
