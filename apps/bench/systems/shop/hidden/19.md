# Step 19 — promotions

R8 stands: a promotion takes a line down to its floor and no further (see
step 18). R7 stands: VAT is worked on what the customer pays for each line
after the promotion.

## The two kinds

- **Buy X, get Y free**, on one product: X and Y are whole numbers of at
  least 1. **HD-100:** for every full group of X + Y units of the product
  on the order, Y units are free. "Buy two, get one free": 3 units cost 2,
  5 units cost 4, 6 units cost 4. The customer has to order the free units;
  the system adds nothing to an order.
- **A set** (bundle): a list of products with a quantity each, and one
  price for the set. **HD-101:** every complete set on the order costs the
  set's price; units beyond complete sets cost their usual price. An order
  with the teapot and four cups, where the set is the teapot and two cups,
  has one set and two cups at the usual price; an order with two teapots
  and four cups has two sets. An order that does not hold a complete set
  gets nothing off.
- What a promotion takes off is shown on the order as one amount, next to
  the discount of a code and what points took off. The total is the value
  minus those three.
- **HD-102, what is refused when a promotion is made:** a product that does
  not exist; X or Y below 1; a set without products, with the same product
  twice, or with fewer than two units in all; a set price that is not
  below what its products cost at their normal prices at that moment (a
  set that is not cheaper is a mistake). A promotion has a name (required).
- **HD-103:** a product can be in one running promotion only. A second
  promotion with a product that is already in a running one is refused.
  Once the first has ended, a new one can be made.
- A promotion runs from the moment it is made until Ola ends it. **HD-104:**
  ending a promotion changes no order that exists: an order placed while it
  ran keeps what it got. Orders placed afterwards get nothing. Ending a
  promotion that has ended is fine and changes nothing.
- Promotions count for everybody: orders with and without an account.
- For a set, what is taken off is split over the lines of the set in
  proportion to their value in the sets (rounding as in HD-89), so each
  line has its amount for VAT and for the floor.

## With codes and points

- **HD-105:** the promotion comes first, then the code, then the points.
  The code's percentage is worked on what is left after the promotion; the
  points can pay for at most half of what is left after the promotion and
  the code (HD-52, HD-55).
- Returns of an order with a promotion follow the rule that is there
  (HD-34): value of the returned lines × total paid / value before any
  discount; the last return gives back the rest.

## Not decided

Promotions with a start and an end date, "the cheapest one free" over
several products, a promotion only for some customers, a limit per
customer.
