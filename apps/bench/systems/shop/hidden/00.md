# What the people at Zielony Kubek know — at the start

You speak for Marta (owner), Kasia (warehouse), Tomek (accounting) and the
security consultant the company hired. Rules marked HD-nn have acceptance
tests tagged with that id.

## Standing requirements (they hold for the whole life of the system)

- **R1, Tomek:** every change of a product's quantity in the warehouse must
  stay on record as a stock movement: how much, why, when, and what it was
  for. The quantity on hand is always the sum of those records. A record is
  never changed or deleted and a quantity is never simply overwritten. Tomek
  must be able to list the stock history of a product, oldest first.
- **R2, Marta:** never sell what is not there. What is available for sale
  (on hand minus what is reserved for orders) can never go below zero.
- **R3, security consultant:** exactly one part of the code may talk to a
  payment provider or know its address. Nothing else calls it.

## Products

- A product has a SKU, a name and a price. The SKU is the key.
- **HD-10:** a SKU is 3 to 32 characters: upper-case letters A–Z, digits
  and dashes. Anything else is refused; a lower-case SKU is refused, not
  corrected.
- **HD-13:** the price is whole cents, above zero. Nothing is free. The
  price can be changed at any time.
- A product can be taken off sale and put back.
- **HD-08:** the product list shows products on sale only, ordered by SKU;
  on request it also shows those off sale, same order.

## Stock

- For each product the system reports on hand, reserved (units on orders
  that are placed or paid and not yet shipped) and available.
- Two kinds of entries made by people: a **receipt** (goods came in) and an
  **adjustment** (correction after a count).
- **HD-06:** a receipt is above zero. An adjustment can be up or down,
  never zero. An adjustment down that would take on hand below what is
  reserved is refused and records nothing.
- **HD-03a:** stock can still be received for a product that is off sale.

## Orders

- An order has the customer's e-mail address (it must look like one) and
  one or more lines: product and quantity.
- Placing an order reserves the stock. If any line cannot be covered the
  whole order is refused and nothing is reserved.
- **HD-07:** quantity per product is 1 to 1000.
- **HD-01:** the same product twice in one order is one line with the
  quantities added (the 1000 limit applies to the sum); lines keep the order
  in which products first appeared.
- A product that does not exist or is off sale cannot be ordered.
- **HD-02:** the price is fixed when the order is placed. A later price
  change does not change the order or what is charged.
- **HD-03b:** taking a product off sale does not touch existing orders;
  they can still be paid and shipped.
- **HD-09:** Marta wants to look up a customer's orders by e-mail address,
  newest first, ignoring upper and lower case. The address is kept as the
  customer typed it.
- States of an order: placed and waiting for payment (pending), paid,
  shipped, cancelled.

## Payments

- Only an order waiting for payment can be paid.
- The charge is the order total. **HD-11:** the `reference` sent to PayLine
  is the order's id, so Tomek can match the statements.
- Every attempt that PayLine answered, succeeded or declined, is kept and
  can be listed for the order.
- Declined: the order keeps waiting and keeps its stock.
- **HD-04:** the third declined payment cancels the order and frees its
  stock. A further attempt is refused.
- **HD-05:** when PayLine itself fails (5xx or no connection) that is not a
  decline: nothing is recorded, it does not count towards the three, and the
  caller is told the provider is unavailable.

## Shipping

- Only a paid order can be shipped.
- **HD-12:** shipping records one stock movement per order line: the goods
  go out, marked as a shipment, with the order's id as what it was for. On
  hand and reserved fall; available does not change.

## Not decided

Concurrent requests, logins, paging, e-mails to customers, and how the API
looks. The developer chooses.

## Not wanted for now

- Taxes: the price of a product is what the customer pays. The system
  works out no VAT and shows none; Tomek does VAT by hand for now and will
  say when that changes. An order's total is the sum of its lines.
- Currencies: everything is in euro.
- Names and postal addresses of customers: an order needs the e-mail
  address and nothing else about the customer. Parcels are addressed by
  hand from the e-mails for now.
