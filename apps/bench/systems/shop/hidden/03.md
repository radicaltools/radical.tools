# Step 3 — unpaid orders

- An unpaid order holds its stock for 15 minutes from the moment it was
  placed. Then it expires: a new final state, different from cancelled.
- **HD-20:** it expires at exactly 15 minutes: when the time is at or past
  the deadline.
- The order must show its deadline while it waits for payment (the web shop
  will show a countdown); an order in any other state has none.
- **HD-21:** a declined payment does not extend the deadline.
- **HD-22:** an expired order cannot be paid and cannot be cancelled.
- The old rule that the third declined payment cancels the order is gone:
  any number of declines leaves the order waiting.
- Only orders waiting for payment expire. Expiry frees the stock and is not
  a stock movement.
- The job that expires orders reports which orders it expired.
