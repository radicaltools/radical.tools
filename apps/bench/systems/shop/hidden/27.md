# Step 27 — a parcel that comes back

R1 stands: what comes back is a stock movement. R10 stands: the goods of a
shipment are the goods of its warehouse, also on the way back. R9 stands:
the invoice of the shipment is not touched; what happened is a credit
note. R6 stands: goods that come back do not change the cost.

- Kasia names the shipment. Everything in it comes back; there is no
  "half a parcel".
- The shipment shows that it failed. A shipment that has not come back is
  simply sent.
- **HD-149:** the goods go back into the warehouse the shipment left from,
  not into another one and not into `MAIN` by default: one stock movement
  per line of the shipment, goods in, marked as a return, with the order's
  id as what it was for. They are free for sale again and fill waiting
  orders like any goods that come in (HD-39).
- The money: Marta does not send a parcel twice. The customer gets the
  money for these goods back, through the payment provider against the
  order's charge, worked out as for a return (HD-34, HD-56); if they still
  want the goods they order again.
- **HD-151:** for the books a failed delivery is a return of everything in
  that shipment: it is on the order's list of returns with its lines and
  its refund, the units count as returned (they cannot be returned a
  second time), and when everything on the order has come back the order
  is returned. An order with another shipment that was delivered stays
  shipped.
- **HD-150:** it makes one credit note, against the invoice of that
  shipment, for the money given back, dated the day the parcel is booked
  as come back (the time the call is told to assume, or today).
- **HD-152:** the shipping cost is not given back (HD-141): the carrier
  has charged us anyway.
- **HD-153:** a shipment comes back once: booking it a second time is
  refused. So is a shipment that does not exist, and a shipment from
  which the customer has already returned goods (it was delivered).
  Nothing is recorded then.
- **HD-154:** when the refund fails at the payment provider nothing
  happens (HD-28): no goods back in stock, the shipment is still sent, no
  credit note and no number used, and the caller is told the provider is
  unavailable.
- **HD-155:** a parcel can come back while the rest of the order has not
  gone out yet. The order stays paid, the rest can still be sent, and the
  order is shipped once that has happened.
- Points follow the money as for a return (HD-50, HD-56).

## Not decided

Sending the same parcel again, charging the customer for the second
postage, the reason a parcel came back, a parcel that comes back damaged,
a parcel that is lost.
