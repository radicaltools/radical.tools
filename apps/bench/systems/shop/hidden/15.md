# Step 15 — what the goods cost

## Standing requirement (it holds for the whole life of the system)

- **R6, Tomek:** the cost of goods is one weighted average per product. It
  changes only when goods are received at a cost. Nothing else moves it:
  not a sale, a shipment, a return, an adjustment, a stock correction, a
  cancelled order or a new selling price. Every change of the average is a
  cost record: how many units came in, at what cost, the average after it,
  and what it was for. A cost record is never changed or deleted, and the
  cost shown on goods that were sold is never recalculated afterwards.
  Tomek must be able to list the cost history of a product, oldest first.

## The average

- Goods received against a purchase order come in at the unit price of
  their purchase order line.
- **HD-75:** new average = (units on hand before the receipt × old average
  + units received × their cost) / (units on hand after), rounded half up
  to a whole cent. "On hand" is all warehouses together, reserved units
  included: there is one average per product, not one per warehouse.
  Example: 4 on hand at 4.00 €, 12 come in at 6.00 €: 5.50 €.
- **HD-76:** when nothing is on hand before the receipt, the new average is
  simply the cost of the receipt; the old average is forgotten.
- **HD-77:** goods received by hand, without a purchase order, can say
  what a unit cost (whole cents above zero); they then count like a
  delivery. When they say nothing, they come in at the current average and
  do not change it, and no cost record is written.
- **HD-79:** a product that was never received at a cost has no average:
  the system shows none (null), not zero, and its stock is worth 0. When
  goods at a cost come in on top of units of unknown cost, the average
  becomes the cost of that receipt.
- The stock value of a product is units on hand × the average.
- Goods that come back from a customer come back at the current average;
  they do not change it. The same for adjustments and corrections.

## What sold goods cost

- **HD-78:** when an order is shipped, each line gets the cost of one unit:
  the product's average at that moment. Before shipping the line shows no
  cost (null). The cost stays on the line for good; later deliveries at
  other prices, and returns, do not change it. (No average known at
  shipping: the line shows none.)
- Tomek works out the margin himself from price and cost.

## Not decided

Costs other than the purchase price (freight, duties), correcting a wrong
purchase price after the delivery was booked, FIFO, a stock value report
over all products.
