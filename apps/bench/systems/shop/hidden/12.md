# Step 12 — blocking a customer, erasing personal data

## Blocking (Jan)

- A blocked customer cannot place orders. The block can be lifted, and then
  they can order again. The account shows whether it is blocked.
- **HD-57:** the block cannot be walked around: an order without an account
  under the blocked account's e-mail address is refused too, upper and
  lower case ignored.
- **HD-58:** a block does not touch what is already there. The customer's
  orders can still be paid, shipped, cancelled and returned; they expire as
  usual; the points stay and still follow the orders.
- **HD-59:** blocking a blocked customer, or unblocking one who is not
  blocked, is fine and changes nothing.
- No reason is recorded for now; Jan keeps that in his own notes.

## Erasing (the data protection consultant)

R4 stands (see step 9), and R5 with it. Erasing means: the person's name,
e-mail address, phone number and addresses are gone from everything the
system shows — the account, the orders, the look-up by e-mail address. The
orders, their lines and amounts, the payments, the returns and the stock
history stay exactly as they were.

- **HD-60:** a customer with unfinished orders cannot be erased yet: an
  order waiting for payment, or paid and not shipped, still needs the
  address. The request is refused and nothing changes. Jan cancels or
  finishes those orders first. Shipped, returned, cancelled and expired
  orders do not stand in the way.
- **HD-61:** the account itself stays, empty: it still has its id, it is
  marked as erased, and name, e-mail address and phone are gone (null). It
  has no addresses. Its orders still say they were on that account. It
  cannot be changed, cannot get addresses and cannot order. Erasing it a
  second time is fine and changes nothing.
- After erasing, the orders show no e-mail address and no shipping address
  (both null), and the look-up of orders by the old e-mail address finds
  none of them. That covers the orders that came onto the account by its
  e-mail address (HD-42) as well.
- **HD-62:** the points are given up, and by the book: what is left of the
  balance is written off with one record (minus the balance, for no order).
  The history stays; it holds no personal data. The balance is then zero,
  and the erased account never gets points again, not even when one of its
  orders is returned later.
- **HD-63:** the e-mail address is free again. A new account can be opened
  with it. It is a new customer: none of the old orders, zero points.
- **HD-64:** goods of an erased customer can still come back: a return on
  one of their shipped orders works as always, with the refund against the
  payment's charge.
- A blocked customer can be erased; the block has nothing left to hold.
