# Step 13 — suppliers and purchase orders

- A supplier has a name (required) and an e-mail address (optional; when
  given it must look like one). The system gives it an id.
- **HD-65:** supplier names are unique, upper and lower case ignored. A
  name of only spaces is no name.
- A purchase order is for one supplier and has one or more lines: product,
  quantity, and the price agreed for one unit, in whole cents. It shows its
  total: the sum of quantity × unit price.
- A purchase order for a supplier or a product that does not exist, or
  without lines, is refused.
- **HD-66:** quantity per line is a whole number from 1 to 10 000. The unit
  price is whole cents above zero; suppliers' free samples are not put on
  purchase orders. The same product twice on one purchase order is refused,
  not added up: Kasia says it is always a typing mistake.
- **HD-67:** a purchase order is for one warehouse, the one the goods will
  be delivered to: `MAIN` unless another is named. A warehouse that does
  not exist: refused.
- **HD-68:** a product that is off sale can still be on a purchase order
  (as it can still be received, HD-03a).
- A purchase order changes nothing in the stock: nothing on hand, nothing
  available, no stock movement. Customers' orders are not served from it.
- The stock figures of a product show how much is on order: the units on
  purchase orders that are open and have not arrived, all warehouses
  together.
- States for now: open, cancelled. A cancelled purchase order no longer
  counts as on order. **HD-69:** cancelling one that is already cancelled is
  fine and changes nothing.
- The purchase orders of a supplier can be listed.
