# Step 25 — carriers and the shipping cost

R7 stands: the shipping cost is whole cents and has VAT of its own, worked
out on that amount and rounded half up. R8 stands and is not touched: the
floor is about the goods. R9 stands: the shipping cost on an invoice is
part of the invoice and never changes.

## Weights, zones, carriers

- A product has a weight: grams per unit, a whole number, zero or more. It
  is optional and can be changed. A product without a weight counts as
  weighing nothing (Kasia will fill the weights in over time).
- A **zone** is a group of countries with a code and a name: "home" is
  `PL`; "EU near" is `DE`, `CZ`, `SK`, …. The zones are the shop's own and
  the same for all carriers. A country is in one zone or in none. The code
  is 2 to 20 characters: upper-case letters, digits and dashes. The zones
  can be listed, each with its countries.
- A **carrier** has a code and a name. For a zone it has prices by weight:
  "up to 1 kg 9.90 €, up to 5 kg 14.90 €". Each such price is: the zone,
  the heaviest parcel it is for (grams), the price in cents. Setting a
  price for the same carrier, zone and weight again replaces it. The
  prices of a carrier can be listed.
- **HD-142, what is refused:** a weight that is not a whole number or is
  below zero; a carrier code that is not 2 to 10 upper-case letters and
  digits, or that is taken; a zone code that is taken, a zone without
  countries, a country that is not a two-letter code in upper case, a
  country that is already in another zone; a price for a zone or a carrier
  that does not exist, for a weight that is not a whole number above zero,
  or below zero. A price of zero is fine (free shipping into a zone).

## The shipping cost of an order

- An order can name a carrier. Then it has a shipping cost; the order
  shows the carrier and the cost, and the total to pay is the goods (after
  everything that was taken off) plus the shipping cost. That total is
  charged.
- An order that names no carrier has no shipping cost and works exactly
  as before (collected in the shop, or agreed by phone). So everything
  that is in the system today stays as it is.
- The parcel is the whole order: its weight is the sum of quantity ×
  weight of the lines. It goes to the country of the order's shipping
  address.
- **HD-133:** the price is that of the carrier's lightest weight limit for
  that zone that the parcel fits under; a parcel that weighs exactly the
  limit fits. A parcel heavier than the carrier's heaviest limit for the
  zone is refused: the carrier does not take it. Nothing is reserved.
- **HD-134:** the order is refused, and nothing is reserved, when the
  carrier does not exist, when the country is in no zone, or when the
  carrier has no prices for the country's zone.
- **HD-135:** an order without a shipping address (an order without an
  account, or one on an account that named no address) goes to the home
  country, `PL`: those are the orders Jan types in for customers on the
  phone, and they are Polish.
- **HD-136:** nothing takes money off the shipping cost. A discount code
  is a percentage of the goods only. Points pay for at most half of the
  goods (what is left of them after promotion and code), not of the
  shipping cost. Promotions and price lists do not touch it.
- **HD-137:** points are collected on the money paid for the goods, not on
  the shipping cost: 30.00 € of goods and 9.90 € shipping give 30 points.
- **HD-138:** the shipping cost is fixed when the order is placed, as the
  prices are. Changing a carrier's prices or a product's weight changes no
  order that exists.
- **HD-139, VAT:** the shipping cost includes VAT at 23 %, whatever is in
  the parcel (Tomek: transport is a service at the standard rate). The VAT
  in it is cost × 23 / 123, rounded half up. The order shows it, and the
  order's VAT is the VAT of its lines plus the VAT of the shipping cost
  (R7). The invoice shows the shipping cost and its VAT as an item of its
  own, next to the lines; the invoice's total and VAT include them.
- **HD-140:** in the daily sales report the shipping cost of the day's
  invoices is in the invoiced figures and in the row for 23 %.
- **HD-141, returns:** the shipping cost is not given back. A return gives
  back money for the goods as before (HD-34), worked on what was paid for
  the goods; an order returned completely has given back the total minus
  the shipping cost, and its credit notes add up to that.

## Not decided

Free shipping above an order value, a parcel split by weight, pick-up
points, tracking numbers, the carrier's own invoice, VAT by destination
country, changing the carrier of an order.
