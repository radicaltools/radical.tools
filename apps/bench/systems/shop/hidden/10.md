# Step 10 — loyalty points, collecting

You now also speak for Ola (marketing).

## Standing requirement (it holds for the whole life of the system)

- **R5, Tomek:** points are money the company owes its customers, so they
  are kept like money. Every change of a customer's balance stays on
  record: how many points, up or down, why, when, and for which order. The
  balance is always the sum of those records. A record is never changed or
  deleted and a balance is never simply overwritten. A balance never goes
  below zero. Tomek must be able to list the points history of a customer,
  oldest first.

## Collecting

- Only orders on an account collect points. An order without an account
  collects nothing (unless it was put on an account by its e-mail address,
  HD-42: then it is that customer's order like any other).
- **HD-47:** one point for every full euro paid for the order, rounded
  down: 25.00 € gives 25 points, 9.99 € gives 9. An order under one euro
  gives nothing, and then nothing is recorded: there are no entries of zero
  points.
- **HD-48:** what counts is what the customer paid: the total after the
  discount code, not the list value.
- **HD-49:** the points are given when the order is paid, not when it is
  placed and not when it is shipped. A declined payment gives nothing. An
  order that is cancelled or expires was never paid, so it gave nothing.
- **HD-50:** a return takes points back. After a return the order keeps
  only the points for the money the customer has not got back: one point
  per full euro of (paid − refunded so far). The difference is taken off the
  balance by a new record for that order. Example: 30.00 € paid, 30 points;
  10.00 € refunded, 10 points are taken back; the rest refunded, the other
  20 go. (R5 still holds: never more is taken than the customer has. That
  cannot happen yet, because points cannot be used.)
- A new account starts with zero points and an empty history.
- Every entry of the history says which order it was for.

## Not decided

Points that expire, levels (silver, gold), points for anything but orders.
