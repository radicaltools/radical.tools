# Step 23 — the daily sales report

R7 stands: every figure in the report is the sum of the rounded figures of
the documents and their lines; nothing is worked out again from a
percentage. R9 stands: the report only reads.

- The report is for one calendar day and is built from the documents dated
  that day: the invoices and the credit notes. It shows how many invoices
  and how many credit notes there were, the invoiced total and the VAT in
  it, the credited total and the VAT in it.
- **HD-121:** it is built from the documents, not from the orders. An order
  that was paid on a day but shipped later counts on the day of its
  invoice. A return counts on the day of its credit note, not on the day
  of the invoice it is against.
- **HD-122:** invoiced and credited are shown side by side, both as
  amounts above zero. The report does not take one off the other; Tomek
  does.
- The split by VAT rate has one row per rate that has something on that
  day: what was invoiced and credited at that rate and the VAT in it,
  added up from the lines of the documents. The rows add up to the totals
  of the report (R7).
- **HD-123:** lines without VAT (a product in no category, or in a category
  at 0 %) are in the split too, in a row for 0 %.
- **HD-124:** a day without documents gives a report of zeros with no rows.
  It is not an error.
- **HD-125:** the day is given as a date, `2026-03-14`. Anything that is
  not a real date is refused.
- The report of a day that is over does not change any more, because the
  documents do not (R9): a return on a later day is on the report of that
  later day.
- The report holds no personal data.

## Not decided

A report over a month, sales per product or per customer group, an export
for the accounting software, the margin.
