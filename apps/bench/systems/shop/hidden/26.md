# Step 26 — a shipment per warehouse, orders that go out in parts

## Standing requirement (it holds for the whole life of the system)

- **R10, Kasia:** a shipment leaves one warehouse and contains only what
  is reserved for that order in that warehouse: never goods of another
  warehouse, never units the order is still waiting for, never more than
  is reserved there. What a shipment says it contains is what left that
  warehouse's stock: one stock movement per product, in that warehouse
  (R1). Whatever is built later around shipping must keep to this. Kasia
  says it when asked about shipments, and it holds from now on.

## Shipments

- A shipment belongs to one order and one warehouse and has lines: product
  and quantity. The shipments of an order can be listed, oldest first.
- **Sending the whole order** stays as it is: a paid order on which
  nothing is backordered is sent. It now makes one shipment per warehouse
  in which the order has goods reserved (in the order of the warehouse
  codes). An order reserved in one warehouse is one shipment, as before.
- **Sending a part:** Kasia names the order and a warehouse; everything
  that is reserved for the order in that warehouse right now goes out as
  one shipment. That is possible while other units are in other
  warehouses or still on backorder. There is no picking of single
  products out of a warehouse's part.
- Units that are reserved later (a backorder filled by a delivery) can go
  out in a further shipment, also from a warehouse that has sent a
  shipment for the order before.
- **HD-143:** the order is shipped when everything on it has gone out.
  Until then it stays paid, and each line shows how many of its units
  have been sent. Sending the whole order is still refused while units are
  on backorder (HD-37); sending a part is the way then. Goods can be
  returned only once the order is shipped completely.
- **HD-148:** sending a part is refused when the order is not paid, when
  the warehouse does not exist, or when nothing is reserved for the order
  there. Nothing leaves, there is no invoice and no number is used (R9).
- The cost of the goods (HD-78) is put on a line when its first units go
  out.

## Invoices

- **HD-144:** one invoice per shipment, made in the same step, for the
  goods in that shipment (as said in step 21). The shipment shows which
  invoice is its own. The date is the day of that shipment (HD-113).
- **HD-145:** the invoices of an order add up to the order (R7). A line
  that goes out in parts is invoiced in parts: the line's amount ×
  units sent / units ordered, rounded down, with VAT worked out on that
  amount; the shipment that completes the line takes what is left of the
  line's amount and of its VAT. Example: 3 units for 29.97 € at 20 %
  (5.00 € VAT), sent one by one: 9.99 € with 1.67 €, 9.99 € with 1.67 €,
  9.99 € with 1.66 €.
- **HD-146:** the shipping cost of the order (step 25) is on the invoice
  of its first shipment, whole. Later invoices carry none.
- An invoice that has been issued is not touched by a later shipment (R9).

## Returns when an order has several invoices

- **HD-147:** a credit note is against the invoice the returned goods
  were on. A return with goods from several invoices makes one credit note
  per invoice, each listing its goods; together they are the money given
  back (the refund is split over the returned goods by their value,
  rounding as in HD-89). A return with goods of one invoice makes one
  credit note, as before.
- When the same product went out in several shipments, returned units
  count against the oldest invoice that still has units of that product
  not credited.
- The credit notes of an order that was returned completely add up to its
  invoices, without the shipping cost, in total and in VAT (HD-120,
  HD-141).

## Not decided

Choosing single products for a shipment, moving a reservation to another
warehouse, a status of its own for "partly shipped", cancelling the rest
of an order that will never be complete, tracking.
