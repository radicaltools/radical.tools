# Step 11 — using points

R5 stands: using points is a record like any other, and nobody can use
points they do not have.

- An order on an account can say how many points to use: a whole number.
  Zero, or leaving it out, means none. An order without an account cannot
  use points: refused.
- **HD-51:** a point is worth 5 cents off the order. (Marta's sum: a point
  per euro, 5 cents per point, 5 % back.)
- **HD-52:** points pay for at most half of an order: their value may not
  be more than half of what is left after the discount code, rounded down
  to a whole cent. Asking for more is refused; the system does not quietly
  use fewer. Marta: "nobody gets an order for free".
- More points than the customer has: refused, nothing reserved, nothing
  recorded.
- The order shows how many points were used and how many cents they took
  off. The total to pay is the value, minus the discount of the code, minus
  the points. That total is what is charged.
- **HD-55:** the code comes first, then the points. Points are collected
  (HD-47) on the money actually paid, so not on the part paid with points.
- **HD-53:** the points leave the balance when the order is placed, not
  when it is paid, so the same points cannot be put on two orders.
- **HD-54:** when the order is cancelled or expires, the points it used
  come back, by a new record for that order.
- **HD-56, returns of an order that used points:** the money back follows
  the rule for discounts (HD-34), with the total paid being the total after
  the points. The points come back the same way: points used × value of the
  returned lines / value before discount, rounded down; the return that
  completes the order gives back whatever is left, so that all points used
  have come back.
- Points an order collected are taken back on a return as before (HD-50).
  Now a customer may have used them already. R5 decides: only as many are
  taken as the customer has; the balance stops at zero.
- In the history, points used are a record below zero for the order; points
  that come back are a record above zero for the same order.

## Not decided

A different value per point for some customers, giving points to someone
else.
