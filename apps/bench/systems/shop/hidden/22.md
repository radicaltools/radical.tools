# Step 22 — credit notes for returns

R9 stands, for credit notes as for invoices: once issued, a credit note is
never changed or deleted, and their numbers run per year without gaps. The
invoice a credit note is against is not touched by it: it stays exactly as
it was issued. R4 stands: a credit note keeps a copy of the buyer, and that
copy goes when the customer is erased.

- The system makes the credit note when a return is booked, in the same
  step: one credit note per return. It names the invoice of the order and
  the order, and lists the products and quantities that came back.
- Its total is the money given back for that return (the refund, HD-34 and
  HD-56), as an amount above zero. A credit note never says something else
  than what the customer got.
- **HD-116:** when the refund fails at the payment provider there is no
  return (HD-28), so there is no credit note and no number is used.
- **HD-117:** credit notes have a series of their own, apart from the
  invoices: `K/2026/0001`, `K/2026/0002`, … (`K/`, the year, a slash, the
  running number with at least four digits), per year from 1. A credit
  note uses no invoice number and an invoice no credit note number.
- **HD-118:** the date of a credit note is the day of the return: the day
  of the time the return call is told to assume, or today (as for
  shipping, HD-113). The year of its number is the year of that date, also
  when the invoice is from the year before.
- **HD-119, the VAT on a credit note:** the money given back is split over
  the returned lines in proportion to their value at the order's prices
  (rounding as in HD-89); each line has the VAT rate it had on the invoice,
  and its VAT is worked out from its share as on an order (HD-85), rounded
  half up; the credit note's VAT is the sum (R7). Example: two mugs at
  12.30 € and 23 %, one comes back: credit note 12.30 €, VAT 2.30 €.
- **HD-120:** the credit note of the return that completes an order takes
  whatever is left of the invoice's VAT, as its total is whatever is left
  of the money (HD-34). All credit notes of a fully returned order add up
  exactly to its invoice, in total and in VAT.
- The buyer on a credit note is the buyer as the invoice shows them at
  that moment. A return for a customer who has been erased (HD-64) gives a
  credit note without a buyer.
- Points that come back with a return (HD-56) are not money and are not on
  the credit note.

## Not decided

A credit note without a return (a price agreed on the phone, an invoice
with a mistake), a new invoice after a correction, a reason on the credit
note.
