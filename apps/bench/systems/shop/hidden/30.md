# Step 30 — the flash sale

R8 stands, and Marta will not give it up: nothing is sold below what it
cost us, sale or no sale. Ola has not looked at the costs; some products
have a margin of far less than 70 %. What Ola needs is a sale that is on
for the whole shop at once, that she switches on and off herself, and
that shows on the order. She does not need the shop to lose money on a
teapot, and she agrees when told. If asked whether the sale may go below
cost: no. Every line gets as much of the 70 % as its floor allows, the
order is accepted, and the order shows what was really taken off.

- A sale is a kind of promotion: a name and a percentage. It counts for
  every product, also for one that is added while the sale runs. Ola ends
  it like any promotion; orders placed while it ran keep what they got
  (HD-104), orders placed afterwards get nothing.
- **HD-168:** per line the sale takes the line's value × the percentage,
  rounded down to a whole cent, and stops at the line's floor (R8, as in
  step 18: the money for the line without its VAT is at least quantity ×
  the average cost when the order is placed). Example: a product at
  12.30 € with 23 % VAT that cost 8.00 €: 70 % would leave 3.69 €; the
  customer pays 9.84 € and the order shows 2.46 € taken off. A product
  whose cost is not known has no floor and gets the full percentage
  (HD-94). A product whose normal price is below its cost gets nothing
  off (HD-99). A line with a good margin does not pay for another line
  (HD-98).
- What the sale took off is in the amount promotions took off on the
  order; VAT is worked on what the customer pays (R7).
- **HD-169:** while a sale runs the other promotions rest: a line gets the
  sale and not its three-for-two or its set. When the sale has ended they
  count again. They can be made and ended while a sale runs.
- **HD-170:** the percentage is a whole number from 1 to 90. One sale runs
  at a time: a second one while the first runs is refused; after the first
  has ended a new one can be made. A sale needs a name.
- Customers of a group get no sale, as they get no promotions (HD-106):
  wholesale prices are the best prices.
- A discount code does not count on a line that got something off from
  the sale (HD-108); an order with a code is still accepted (HD-109).
- Points go with the sale (HD-111): at most half of what is left, and no
  line below its floor (HD-96: an order that asks for more is refused).
- The shipping cost is not part of the sale (HD-136).

## Not decided

A sale that starts and ends by itself at a given time, a sale for a
category only, products left out of a sale, a different percentage per
product, showing the old price crossed out.
