# Step 4 — fixing stock from an order

R1 stands, and Tomek will not give it up: the books need every change on
record. Kasia only wants one action and no arithmetic; she does not care
how it is stored. If asked whether the number may simply be overwritten:
no. The system works out the difference itself and records it as an
adjustment.

- **HD-24:** that adjustment is recorded with the order's id as what it was
  for, in the warehouse being corrected (`MAIN` unless another is named).
- **HD-25:** when the counted number equals the current one, nothing is
  recorded.
- **HD-23:** a correction that would take on hand in that warehouse below
  what is reserved there is refused; nothing is recorded.
- **HD-26:** the product must be on the order, otherwise refused. The order
  can be in any state.
- The counted number is a whole number, zero or more.
