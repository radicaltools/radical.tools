# Step 30 — the flash sale

Ola needs a sale that is on for the whole shop at once, that she switches
on and off herself, and that shows on the order. She has not looked at the
costs and knows of no rules about prices.

- A sale is a kind of promotion: a name and a percentage. It counts for
  every product, also for one that is added while the sale runs. Ola ends
  it like any promotion; orders placed while it ran keep what they got,
  orders placed afterwards get nothing.
- Per line the sale takes the line's value × the percentage, rounded down
  to a whole cent. What the sale took off is in the amount promotions took
  off on the order.
- **HD-169:** while a sale runs the other promotions rest: a line gets the
  sale and not its three-for-two or its set. When the sale has ended they
  count again. They can be made and ended while a sale runs.
- **HD-170:** the percentage is a whole number from 1 to 90. One sale runs
  at a time: a second one while the first runs is refused; after the first
  has ended a new one can be made. A sale needs a name.
- Customers of a group get no sale.
- A discount code does not count on a line that got something off from
  the sale; an order with a code is still accepted.
- The shipping cost is not part of the sale.

## Not decided

A sale that starts and ends by itself at a given time, a sale for a
category only, products left out of a sale, a different percentage per
product, showing the old price crossed out.
