# Step 28 — an invoice with the wrong amount

Jan only wants one action: he names the invoice and the amount that was
agreed, the customer gets the difference back, and the customer's papers
then say what they really paid. He does not care how the books show it and
knows of no rules about it.

- "The amount of the invoice" that Jan corrects is what is left of it: its
  total minus what was given back against it already (returns, earlier
  corrections). The amount agreed replaces that.
- The difference goes back to the customer through the payment provider,
  against the order's charge, as a refund does.
- **HD-157, VAT:** the difference is split over the goods on the invoice in
  proportion to what is left on each line; each share has the VAT rate of
  its line, its VAT is worked out from the share and rounded half up.
  Example: a mug at 12.30 € (23 %) and tea at 21.00 € (5 %), agreed
  29.97 €: 3.33 € goes back, of which 1.23 € on the mug with 0.23 € VAT
  and 2.10 € on the tea with 0.10 € VAT.
- **HD-158:** only downwards. An amount above what is left of the invoice
  is refused. An amount equal to what is left is fine and changes nothing.
  An amount below zero or not in whole cents is refused; so is an invoice
  that does not exist. The shipping cost on an invoice is not corrected: an
  amount below it is refused.
- **HD-159:** when the refund fails at the payment provider nothing
  happens, and the caller is told the provider is unavailable.
- **HD-160:** an invoice can be corrected more than once, and after a
  return; each time the amount agreed replaces what is left.
- **HD-161, returns afterwards:** the customer has now paid less for the
  goods, and a return never gives back more than they paid. The return
  that completes the order gives back whatever is left.
- **HD-162:** after a correction the order keeps one point per full euro
  still paid for the goods; the rest is taken back. Points that were used
  on the order do not come back.
- The correction changes no stock and no cost. It is on the daily sales
  report of its day as credited.

## Not decided

A reason on the correction, corrections upwards, correcting the buyer's
name or address on an invoice, who may correct.
