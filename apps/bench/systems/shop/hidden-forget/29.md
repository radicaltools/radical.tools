# Step 29 — a customer who wants to be gone

Marta wants two things: **nothing about the woman is left anywhere in the
system**, and **it is done in one go**, without Jan having to clear the
customer's open orders first. She knows of no rules about what must be
kept.

- The e-mail address is free afterwards: whoever opens an account with it
  is a new customer. Nothing is found by the old e-mail address.
- **HD-163:** an order waiting for payment is cancelled, like any
  cancelled order: its stock is free again.
- **HD-164:** an order that is paid and of which nothing has gone out is
  called off: the customer gets all of its money back through the payment
  provider (the whole total, shipping cost included) and its stock is free
  again.
- **HD-165:** an order of which a part has gone out and the rest has not
  cannot be called off: the removal is refused and nothing changes. Jan
  waits for the rest to go out, then removes the customer.
- **HD-166:** when the payment provider fails during a refund, the removal
  stops and the caller is told the provider is unavailable. The customer
  is still there with their data. Doing it again goes on from there.
- Removing a customer who was removed before is fine and changes nothing.
  A customer that does not exist: refused. A blocked customer can be
  removed. A removed customer cannot order.

## Not decided

A confirmation sent to the customer, removing after a retention period,
removing customers who never had an account, an export of the data before
it goes.
