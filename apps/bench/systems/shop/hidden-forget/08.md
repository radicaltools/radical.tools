# Step 8 — selling what is not on the shelf

Piotr wants the order taken even when the goods are not all there. He does
not care how the missing units are counted and knows of no rules about it.

- An order for more than is available is accepted.
- **HD-37:** such an order can be paid. It can be shipped only when all of
  its goods are there for it.
- **HD-39:** when goods come in or come free (a receipt, a correction up, a
  return, a cancelled or expired order) the orders that wait get them,
  oldest order first, in the warehouse where the goods appeared.
- Cancelling or expiring an order ends its waiting for goods.
