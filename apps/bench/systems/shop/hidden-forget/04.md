# Step 4 — fixing stock from an order

Kasia only wants one action and no arithmetic: she names the order, the
product and the number she counted, and that is what is on hand. She does
not care how it is stored and knows of no rules about it.

- The correction is for one warehouse (`MAIN` unless another is named).
- When the counted number equals the current one, nothing changes.
- **HD-23:** a correction that would take on hand in that warehouse below
  what is reserved there is refused.
- **HD-26:** the product must be on the order, otherwise refused. The order
  can be in any state.
- The counted number is a whole number, zero or more.
