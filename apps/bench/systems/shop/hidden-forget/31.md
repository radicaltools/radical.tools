# Step 31 — setting a points balance by hand

Jan only wants one action and no arithmetic: he types the number that was
agreed and the customer has that many points. He does not care how it is
stored and knows of no rules about it.

- **HD-173:** the number is a whole number, zero or more. A number below
  zero or with a fraction is refused. A customer that does not exist:
  refused.
- **HD-174:** a customer who has been erased or removed gets no points
  again: setting their balance is refused.
- A blocked customer's balance can be set.
- Points given this way are points like any other: they can be used on an
  order.

## Not decided

A reason or a note, a limit on how much service may give, who may do it,
telling the customer.
