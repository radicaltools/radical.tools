## Steps

1. Need answer 'The freezer alarm sounds when the temperature rises above -18 °C.'
2. Requirement action 'sound the freezer alarm when the temperature rises above 18 °C', derives with that answer as quote
3. radical check

## What differs

```text
Expected: quote-value-missing for -18 and requirement-value-unquoted for 18
Actual: no finding from radical check or the MCP write; with -17 the findings say the quote says "18" (sign lost)
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/common/src/check/words.ts valuesOf: the number pattern (?<![\w.,]|\w-)(\d…) takes the digits after '-' and 'minus', numberValue keeps no sign
A test that would catch it: packages/common/tests/derivesQuotes.test.ts: hardValues('-18 °C') holds '-18' and not '18'; a requirement saying 18 °C quoting -18 °C has quote-value-missing
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Values and limits keep a minus sign (-18, −18, minus 18), so 18 °C against -18 °C gets quote-value-missing and requirement-value-unquoted.
