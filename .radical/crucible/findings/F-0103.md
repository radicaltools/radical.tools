## Steps

1. Sample, Designer, Payment lifecycle: Milestones, + New milestone 'M'
2. Select the Payment lifecycle state machine, Delete, Remove from model
3. Presenter: Milestones, M; Views, State machines, Payment lifecycle (9 drawn); Add slide
4. Milestones: Current (live); F5, go to the last slide

## What differs

```text
Expected: Slide 13 draws the 9 elements of Payment lifecycle as milestone M has them, from the live model and after a reload too
Actual: Saved slide {viewId: view-payment-lifecycle, snapshotId: M}; F5 with M picked: 9 drawn; F5 from Current (live): 0 drawn, the All elements table; after a reload: 0 drawn, the table
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: guess: diagramStore goToSlide/previewSlide: slideViewIdOf(slide, get().views) looks the view up in the live views only; the slide's milestone's own views (DiagramSnapshot.views, milestoneOnlyView) are never shown for it
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests/milestonePresenting.test.ts: delete a group subject live, selectMilestone(M), setActiveView(its view), addPresentationSlide, back to live, startPresentation + goToSlide(last): activeViewId is that view and the subject's children are drawn
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

A playing slide shows its milestone's views, so a slide of a view the milestone has alone draws its elements from the live model and after a reload.
