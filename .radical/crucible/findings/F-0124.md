## What differs

```text
Expected: Either the import shows, or the Welcome screen says the packs wait for a model to be chosen
Actual: 'Import from Hub' and 'Authentication — concern' are open under the Welcome overlay (z-index 9999 vs 9000/9100); elementFromPoint at their centre is the Welcome screen; Escape closes the concept dialog; after opening a model from Recent, 'Import from Hub' shows
```

## Decision (2026-11-15)
