## Steps

1. Bookstore fixture, System Context in Designer
2. Drag Payment Provider a little and click it (it is pinned: the Unpin pin shows)
3. Export → Export as SVG… / PNG
4. Select Bookstore, Alt-click Payment Provider, Align… → Keep in a row, click Bookstore
5. Export → Export as SVG… / PNG

## What differs

```text
Expected: The picture shows the elements and their relations only, like the discussion icons and exploration controls left out since F-0067
Actual: Pinned + selected: exported SVG has 1 alignment-guide-btn (aria-label="Unpin"), the PNG shows the pin at the corner. Row kept, member selected: SVG has the alignment-guides layer and 2 alignment-guide-btn ('Stop keeping row order', 'Remove row alignment'); the PNG shows the dashed guide and the → and × buttons over the relation label. The selection outline and Focus dimming are drawn too
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/studio/src/renderer/src/hooks/useExport.ts EDITING_CONTROLS has no alignment-guide entries; AlignmentGuides.tsx assumes exports capture only the viewport, but the target is .canvas-area
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/export.spec.ts: pin an element by a drag, select it, keep two in a row, Export as SVG and assert the SVG contains no 'alignment-guide'
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

Every editing control carries data-editing-control and the export drops whatever carries it (one rule instead of a list), and the export draws no selection or Focus dimming; a container's −/+ and the milestone chip's buttons are left out too (IMPROVEMENTS item 291).
