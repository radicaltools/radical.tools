## Steps

1. bookstore plus 30 views 'More 00..29' showing Customer, open System Context in Designer
2. Click Milestones on the activity rail
3. Select Customer, in the card's Views click 'More 29'
4. Click Views on the activity rail

## What differs

```text
Expected: The active card 'More 29' is in sight in the list (as with Views left open: scrollTop 1164, card at y 616-662)
Actual: Card at y 1780-1826, list y 88-720, scrollTop 0; the same when the view is opened from Quick Search
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/components/RightPanel.tsx ViewList: the reveal effect calls scrollIntoView while the section is hidden (display none) and clears toReveal anyway; a guess
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/show-in-view.spec.ts: as 'a view opened from the card is scrolled into sight in the list of views' but with Milestones open first, then Views clicked; expect the active card toBeInViewport
```

## Fixed

2026-10-22 (fix round of round 170–175, branch fix/r170): The list of views scrolls the opened view into sight once Views is the open section, not while it is hidden.
