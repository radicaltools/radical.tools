## Steps

1. Seed a view with systems A, B, C, X, Y, D, E (X uses Y, A uses X; D and E unrelated, D 20 px above E)
2. Select A, B, C → Align… → Keep in a row
3. Drag D 20 px to the right (it is pinned)
4. Double-click X → Tidy around

## What differs

```text
Expected: Only X and what it relates to (Y, A and A's row) move; D stays where it was dropped and keeps its pin, E stays
Actual: D (pinned) moves (0, -8) and E (0, +8): their 24 px gap is opened to 40. Without the row neither moves. On the injury model's Conceptual view (70 elements, row of 3 requirements): pinned 'Czerwone flagi' moves (0, 5), unrelated 'Interpret free text' (0, -13) and 'Movement test question' (0, 21)
Seen: 3 of 3 small model, 3 of 3 injury model
Where it probably comes from: packages/layout/src/tidy/index.ts runTidyLayout: lineUp() and the closing enforceAlignments call constraints.ts enforceAlignments, whose separate() pushes every sibling pair closer than ROOT_GAP apart, ignoring pinned elements and the around set (engine alone reproduces it: eng/around-align.mts)
A test that would catch it: packages/layout/tests/tidy/craft.test.ts: runTidyLayout with around:[X], pinned:[D], alignments:[row A,B,C], D and E 10 px apart: D and E keep their positions
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

Tidy lines up the view's rows only where a line is off, and its separate pass never pushes pinned elements or elements outside a Tidy-around.
