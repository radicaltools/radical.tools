## Steps

1. A project with crucible.json and a prepared round with run 1
2. run-1/bug-no.mjs prints 'BUG: no undo step is made for the drag' and exits 1
3. radical explore verify 1 bug-no.mjs

## What differs

```text
Expected: 'the bug shows'; round.json shown: true
Actual: 'the bug does not show, as its output says; its exit 1 says otherwise'; round.json shown: false for 'BUG: no …' and 'FINDING: 0 …', true for 'BUG: the drag made two undo steps'
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/cli/src/explore.ts SAYS_NO /^\s*\w+\s*[:=]?\s*(no|false|none|0)\b/i applies to BUG and FINDING lines too, not only REPRODUCED
A test that would catch it: apps/cli/src/explore.test.ts: judgeScript with exit 1 and 'BUG: no undo step' or 'FINDING: 0 of 3 …' says the bug shows
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Explore verify takes every BUG/FINDING line as the bug showing; only REPRODUCED: no says it does not.
