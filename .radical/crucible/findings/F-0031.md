## Steps

1. Open the bookstore fixture in Designer, Metamodel editor, Queue card
2. Untick every allowed parent and At the root
3. Close the editor; drag Queue from the palette onto the canvas, then into Bookstore

## What differs

```text
Expected: Leaving a type with no place is asked about or warned of; failing that, the refusal says the type may not be placed anywhere
Actual: 0 dialogs, Validation 0, stored allowedParents undefined and allowedAtRoot false, Queue still in the palette; drop at root: 'Queue must be placed inside a parent.'; drop into Bookstore: 'Cannot place Queue inside Software System. Allowed parents: the canvas root.' MCP accepts upsert_node_type queue {allowedParents: [], allowedAtRoot: false}; add_node at the root: 'Cannot place Queue inside the canvas root.
```
