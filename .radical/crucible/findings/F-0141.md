## Steps

1. Open a folder model with a view 'Every requirement' whose filter gives 3,000 requirements, in Designer: the notice 'Every requirement shows 3,000 elements. A canvas draws up to 1,000' is shown
2. Click Edit the filter
3. Click View type: Explore

## What differs

```text
Expected: The notice stays (or Explore is refused or capped for a view of more than 1,000); no more than 1,000 elements on a canvas
Actual: 3,000 .react-flow__node drawn, no notice, Smart Layout offered; on the repository's model x2 a blank view switched to Explore drew 3,978 elements and took 34 s to settle
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts oversizedCanvas returns null for isExploration(view); packages/ui/src/store/exploration.ts explorationOf lists every element of the view (guess: the + (All) of Explore from here has no cap either)
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests/oversizedCanvas.test.ts: a view whose chain gives 1,001 elements, setViewKind(id, 'explore') -> oversizedCanvas(state) is not null (or the switch is refused); apps/e2e/tests/studio/large-canvas.spec.ts: Edit the filter -> Explore keeps the notice and 0 .react-flow__node
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Explorations now count toward the 1,000-element canvas limit (canvasOverflow), a view over the limit cannot be switched to Explore (button disabled, notice stays), and a + that would pass the limit adds nothing.
