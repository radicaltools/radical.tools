## Steps

1. Sample, Domain model (draws entities only; domains are undrawn frames)
2. Click Payment, hold Alt, drag onto the empty canvas, release
3. Or the same with Fraud Case, then open Fraud case lifecycle

## What differs

```text
Expected: Payment stays where it stood (nothing drawn is in its way); on the lifecycle views, which do not draw the domain either, it does not move
Actual: Payment y 470.4 -> 870.4 on Domain model and 120 -> -120 on Payment lifecycle; Fraud Case stays on Domain model but moves 120 -> -120 on Fraud case lifecycle, clear of the undrawn Fraud & Risk frame
Seen: 2 of 2 (payment), 2 of 2 (fraudcase)
Where it probably comes from: guess: packages/common/src/model.ts reparentNodes: `taken` holds every root sibling with a place in the view's positions, undrawn frames included; packages/layout/src/geometry.ts settleMovedNodes freeSpotNear likely counts frames too
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests/reparentOnEveryCanvas.test.ts: an entity taken out of its domain on a view whose chain draws entities only keeps its place there and on a lifecycle view that draws it without the domain
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

A view's invisible frames no longer count as obstacles when an element is taken out to the top, on the canvas shown, on the other canvases and after Smart Layout; what a frame holds still does.
