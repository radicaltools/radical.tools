## Decision (2026-11-15)

Every writer of .radical/radical.json keeps the keys it does not know, top-level and on entries ($schema, owner, a newer version's keys): a parse/serialize round trip changes only what the command changes. To be written into the model's requirement on the branch that fixes it.

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Parse and serialize keep unknown keys top-level, in model/tests/ci and on entries, and every writer merges a changed entry over the old one.
