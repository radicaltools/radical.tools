## Steps

1. Bookstore fixture, an AI provider set, Designer on System Context
2. Open Radical Forge (the search bar's Forge button)
3. Click Payment Provider beside the panel and press Delete — or Models → Manage models…, or Settings → AI providers…
4. Try to click a button of the dialog

## What differs

```text
Expected: The dialog opened last is on top of the Forge panel and can be used
Actual: Delete prompt: 3 of 3 buttons under the Forge panel (Remove from model, Hide from current view, Cancel); Models dialog 6 of 6; AI providers 8 of 8; with the agent's Assay panel open, the Models dialog 6 of 6
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/studio/src/renderer/src/studio.css .forge-panel z-index 4000 (Radical Forge, agent Forge and Assay panels) above the Models backdrop (2000, studio.css:1102), the AI providers and About dialogs and .milestone-modal
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/forge.spec.ts: with Forge open, select an element outside the panel, press Delete; elementFromPoint at the Cancel button's centre is inside .milestone-modal; same for the Models dialog's Close
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Dialogs and run panels share one layer in the body, so the one opened last is on top, and Esc closes only the one on top (keysOnTop).
