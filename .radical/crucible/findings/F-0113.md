## Steps

1. Repository with scenario 'Pays by card' and src/pay.test.ts
2. radical verify "Pays by card" --test "src/pay.test.ts > pay > takes a card"
3. radical check --report vitest.json (the test passed)
4. radical check --trace pay

## What differs

```text
Expected: The same as with ›: Scenarios: 1 passed, Requirements: 1 verified, trace says passed
Actual: With '>': test-not-run warning, Scenarios: 1 not-run, Requirements: 1 unverified, trace not-run; with '›': 1 passed, 1 verified (Vitest 4.1.11 prints ' > ')
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/common/src/check/reports.ts parseTestIds splits on /\s+›\s+/ only (splitTestId in testSource.ts and JUNIT_SEPARATOR accept >); apps/cli/src/verify.ts and MCP set_verification store the id as given
A test that would catch it: packages/common/tests check: a bound 'a.test.ts > d > t' finds a Vitest JSON case as 'a.test.ts › d › t' does; or apps/cli/src/verify.test.ts: verify stores the › form
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Test ids are read with either › or Vitest's >, and radical verify, set_verification and harden --test keep or accept the › form.
