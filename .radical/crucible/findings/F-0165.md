## Steps

1. Payments with orders fetched; orders has a wiki view of every element ('Orders wiki')
2. Open → Folder… on payments, Designer, Views → orders › Orders wiki
3. Click Multi page

## What differs

```text
Expected: The wiki shows Multi page (one article, children as chapters), as on the model's own wikis
Actual: Multi page button at (699, 69) 74×18 under the bar at (416, 56) 752×28; elementFromPoint at its middle is the bar; after the click the wiki is still on Single page. Same in Designer at 1600 and 1920 px wide, and in Viewer at 1280
Seen: 4 of 4
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/components/OtherModelViewBar.tsx placed over the view's own header (.other-model-bar in packages/ui/src/index.css); on the hierarchy it also covers 'Size by' and on the matrix its title (guess)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/federation.spec.ts: on another model's wiki, a click on Multi page makes it active (and no control of a view's header lies under .other-model-bar)
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

The read-only bar of another model's view has a grid row of its own above the view, so it covers none of the view's controls on any view kind.
