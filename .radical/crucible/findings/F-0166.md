2. Designer: open view Payments
3. Inspector › Concern unaddressed › Authentication (Storefront) › Answer the questions… › Add to model

## What differs


## Fixed (2026-11-15)

A concern pack imported from the Inspector, Viewer or Presenter is listed only in its own Concerns view, never in the view Designer has open, and Designer opens that view when Studio stays there.
