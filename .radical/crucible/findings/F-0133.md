## Steps

1. Shop folder, Viewer, Quick Search 'Check 3-D Secure' (verified) › Path
2. Tick 'Only what does not pass'
3. Same for scenario 'Paying by card' (passed)

## What differs

```text
Expected: The passing scenario and test are left out, or kept, but nothing says the element has none
Actual: Column 4 under '✓ REQUIREMENT verified Check 3-D Secure' becomes the gap card 'no scenario verifies it'; under '✓ SCENARIO passed Paying by card' it becomes 'names no check'
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/studio/src/renderer/src/components/inspector/TracePanel.tsx: onlyFailing keeps the target with its passing children and results removed, and layout() calls traceGap() on the emptied node
A test that would catch it: apps/studio/tests/tracePanel.test.ts (export onlyFailing/layout) or apps/e2e/tests/studio/tracePath.spec.ts: with 'Only what does not pass' on a passing scenario, no .trace-gap is drawn
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

'Only what does not pass' keeps each element's gap from the whole path and draws no false gap.
