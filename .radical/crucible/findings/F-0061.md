## Steps

1. Sample model, System Context, Designer, Focus on (default)
2. single-click Core Banking Platform (no card opens)
3. compare the framed elements with the canvas right of the palette; then double-click it (card opens) for comparison

## What differs

```text
Expected: with no card open, the selection and its neighbours framed in the canvas right of the palette (x 484..1280 at 1280x720, 484..1600 at 1600x900), nothing under the palette
Actual: 1280x720: card closed, framing falls back to the whole canvas, SWIFT Network 39 of 178 px under the palette (x 445 < 484); 1600x900: framed x 548..1186, 64 px from the palette, 414 px empty on the right where no card is; Viewer the same (about 400 px empty on the right)
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/components/propertiesCover.ts propertiesPanelCover counts the card when one element is selected (`one`), though since #251 a click no longer opens it; used by Canvas.tsx viewportInFree (frameNodes) and the 'already in view' check in frameSelection
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/selection-focus.spec.ts: at 1280x720 and 1600x900 a single click on Core Banking on System Context leaves the card closed and every non-dimmed node's box right of the palette's right edge, with left and right margins within ~100 px of each other
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

propertiesPanelCover counts the properties card only while it is open or asked for (double-click, Quick Search), so a click frames the selection in the canvas the palette leaves free.
