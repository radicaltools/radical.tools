## Steps

1. add_node need with two answers; add_node requirement 'keep at most 25 items in a basket'; add_relation derives quoting 'A basket holds at most 20 items.' (reports 2 findings)
2. update_node requirement label only
3. update_node need label only
4. control: update_node rationale

## What differs

```text
Expected: A rename brings in nothing: no 'The person's words … after this change' list
Actual: requirement rename repeats its 2 findings; need rename repeats 2 (one of them the unquoted answer that was there from the start); rationale update reports none
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/mcp/src/folderModel.ts wordsBroughtIn: findings before and after are compared by `${rule}\0${message}`, and messages carry the labels
A test that would catch it: apps/mcp/src/folderModel.test.ts: after a write that reports word findings, update_node of the requirement's or need's label alone reports no word findings
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

WordsBroughtIn compares findings by rule, element or relation id and message, reading the model before under the new labels, so a rename brings in nothing.
