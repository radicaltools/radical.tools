## Steps

1. Sample, Domain model: select Payment
2. Hold Alt, drag onto the empty canvas, release (or card Parent: —)

## What differs

```text
Expected: Payment stays where it stood, or goes where it was dropped
Actual: Payment (615.6,470.4) -> (1115.6,-51.2), released at (1188,829), the others spanning x -85..1006, y 69..712; kept after a reload. Screen Map, Payment On Hold out of the drawn group Customer journey: (-292.8,1975.7) -> (2492,-1082.9), outside the others' span x -584..2399, y -963..2174
Seen: 4 of 4 (domain, key and card), 2 of 2 (screens)
Where it probably comes from: guess: model.reparentNodes / settleMoveOnShownCanvas -> settleMovedNodes: at the top the element lies over its former container, now a sibling, so its place is dropped and it is placed beside everything
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/nesting.spec.ts: Alt take-out of an entity on view-domain-model and Parent '—' leave it within 1 px or at the drop point
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

At the top of the model a moved element goes to the free place nearest where it stood, on the canvas shown (settleMovedNodes) and on the others (model.reparentNodes), instead of to the right of every root element.
