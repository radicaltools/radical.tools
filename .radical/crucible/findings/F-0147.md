## Steps

1. A prepared round with run 1 and run-1/f1-drag.mjs
2. radical explore verify 1 f1-dragg.mjs
3. Or: round-3-4 exists; radical explore prepare --runs 2 --from 4 --no-build (exit 0, round-4-5); put f2-pin.mjs into round-4-5/run-4; radical explore verify 4 f2-pin.mjs

```text

Actual: Both: node 'Cannot find module …/run-1/f1-dragg.mjs' (and …/round-3-4/run-4/f2-pin.mjs), exit 1, '→ the bug shows (exit 1)', round.json shown: true; prepare --from 4 exit 0 with run 4 in two rounds (findRun takes the older)
```
