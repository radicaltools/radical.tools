## Steps

1. Sample model, + New view → Blank view (a table)
2. Filter: + Start with Entity, + Level under 'Made up of → Entity'
3. + Entity · made up of under Payment (an aggregate root): Existing lists Account, Beneficiary, Customer, Fraud Case, Ledger Entry, Payment Batch
4. pick Account

## What differs

```text
Expected: Only entities that may be part of Payment are offered (as the card's Type select, the wiki's other relationships and Alt-drag do with relationTypeKeepsAggregates), or the link is refused with the reason
Actual: 6 of 6 offered break the aggregate rules; Account part-of Payment is added with no notice and Validation goes 0 -> 1 ('Aggregate root "Account" is part of "Payment"…'); Beneficiary gives 'is part of 2 aggregates'. Over all the sample's chains and viewpoints, 53 of 10,524 candidates offered are then flagged and 37 always refused
Seen: 3 of 3
Where it probably comes from: packages/common/src/viewPipeline.ts linkCandidates checks only relationTypesFitting, not relationTypeKeepsAggregates or checkAddRelation (a guess: WikiView's Add or link uses it too)
A test that would catch it: packages/common/tests/viewPipeline.test.ts: every linkCandidates candidate of every addChoice on the fintech sample passes checkAddRelation and adds no validateModel issue; apps/e2e/tests/studio/view-pipeline.spec.ts: Entity / Made up of table, the picker under Payment lists no aggregate root
```

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

A part-of that breaks the aggregate rules is refused with the reason and no longer offered.
