## Steps

1. Load modelling-cut.json (cut from radical.tools' own .radical/model; the view Modelling of the group 'Entities of a domain' has no positions of its own)
2. Open it in Viewer on the view Modelling
3. Touch nothing and watch the canvas for 10 s

## What differs

```text
Expected: The view is drawn once at All elements' places (as Viewer shows a view never laid out) and stays still
Actual: All 12 elements move every second from 1 s to 8 s after opening, the farthest about 7,100 px between 1 s and rest; then still. In Designer the same view gets its first Smart Layout at once and stays still. In the repository's own model 440 of 1,973 children lie outside their parent in All elements' places (up to 60,000 px), and the live physics pulls them in. The sample's views do not move
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: The live layout (packages/layout/src/liveColaEngine.ts, started by startLiveLayout in diagramStore.ts) relaxes a canvas opened on All elements' places whose children lie outside their frames; arrangeOnFirstOpen skips Viewer (guess)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio: a view per element with no positions, whose subject's children lie outside its frame in defaultPositions, opened in Viewer: every element's transform is the same at 1 s and at 8 s
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Elements placed as a canvas opens settle only in Designer (settlesOnOpen), so Viewer and Presenter stand still.
