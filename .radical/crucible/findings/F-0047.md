## Steps

1. Open the sample on Payments Domain
2. Open Core Banking, double-click Fraud Detection, card Actions → Wrap into… → Group
3. Open Payments Domain from the Views panel

## What differs

```text
Expected: Payments Domain stays as it was (at most Fraud Detection takes its place in the new group)
Actual: 10 of 15 moved/resized: Core Banking Platform x 220→23, w 1315→1634; Banking Services x 905→1091; Risk & Data w 520→912, y 663→891; Accounts Service, Event Bus, Payments Service and its two components, Transactions DB, Fraud Detection (280,783→518,1061). The new group is not drawn there but gets a saved position; the new places are saved. Undo of the wrap restores Payments Domain exactly. A special-key nest into an existing group moves only Fraud Detection there
Seen: 4 of 4
Where it probably comes from: guess: the wrapper has no position on that view; on opening it is given one and freed (LiveColaEngine.reset(free) → rebuild 'local'), so the physics moves its 40 nearest elements
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/editing.spec.ts: wrap ctn-fraud on view-core, open view-payments; every element there keeps its transform within 1 px
```

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

Wrap into… puts the new parent round the wrapped elements on every other canvas where they have places (tightly where it is a frame), so a view that does not draw it stays exactly as it was.
