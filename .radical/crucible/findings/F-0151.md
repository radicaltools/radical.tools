## Steps

1. Need answer 'The freezer alarm sounds when the temperature rises above –18 °C.' (U+2013)
2. Requirement 'sound the freezer alarm when the temperature rises above 18 °C', derives with that answer as quote; radical check
3. Requirement '… above -18 °C' instead; radical check

## What differs

```text
Expected: –18 is read as -18, as U+2212 and the hyphen are: dropping the sign gives quote-value-missing, copying it gives nothing
Actual: drops the sign: 0 findings (with a hyphen in the need: quote-value-missing + requirement-value-unquoted); copies -18: quote-value-missing "18" and requirement-value-unquoted "-18". The limits read the same text as above -18.
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/common/src/check/words.ts valuesOf: the sign class [-−] has no en dash (plainWords maps every dash to '-', which is why limitsOf reads -18)
A test that would catch it: packages/common/tests/derivesQuotes.test.ts › reading the person's words: hardValues('above –18 °C') holds '-18'; a requirement saying 18 °C quoting –18 °C has quote-value-missing
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Values read every minus dash (U+2212, en dash, figure dash, other hyphens) as a minus, as the limits do, and a dash after a number is a range whose second number is read.
