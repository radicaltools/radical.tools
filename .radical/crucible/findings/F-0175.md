2. MCP align_nodes { viewId: view-core, axis: horizontal, nodeIds: [comp-fx, comp-validator] }

## What differs

```text
Expected: FX Engine and Payment Validator on one line side by side (as with around: [comp-fx]: x 20 and 240), Payments Service, out of the selection's reach, at its place and size
Actual: Both saved at (20, 120) 200x120 in Payments Service, drawn one on the other in Studio (canvas (952, 662) for both); Payments Service refit 300x396 -> 300x260 though out of reach. The same for around: [ctn-auth] and, with dom-access pinned, [ctn-db-tx]. Studio's Tidy around Fraud Detection on the same file: no overlap, 11 of 14 positions differ from the MCP result
Seen: 2 of 2 (plus 2 variants of around)
Where it probably comes from: packages/layout/src/tidy/index.ts lineUp() -> constraints.ts enforceAlignments(nodes, positions, alignments, fixed): snap() puts fixed/out-of-reach members on the line, separate() never pushes fixed elements (F-0106 fix), so they stay overlapped; fit() refits frames out of reach (guess)
A test that would catch it: packages/layout/tests/tidy/craft.test.ts: runTidyLayout with around: [X] (no relation to the row), alignments: [row A, B] where A and B are one above the other in container P: A and B do not overlap afterwards, and P's position and size are as given; apps/mcp/src/folderModel.test.ts: align_nodes then smart_layout around an unrelated element gives no overlapping siblings
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Tidy no longer moves onto a line a member that is pinned or out of reach (or carries one along with its line), and the MCP server lines up an agent-set row before smart_layout as Studio does on showing the canvas, so the two members end side by side and Studio and MCP give the same layout (0 of 14 positions differ).
