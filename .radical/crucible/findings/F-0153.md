## Steps

1. Need answer "A guest who can't pay yet keeps at most 20 items in the basket."
2. Requirement 'keep at most 20 items in the basket of a guest', derives quoting the answer
3. radical check (or read the add_relation answer)

## What differs

```text
Expected: No finding: both say at most 20
Actual: quote-value-differs for 3 of 3 phrasings (can't pay, doesn't log in, cannot pay): 'allows up to and with 20 ("at most 20") where the words it quotes allow above 20 ("at most 20")'; control 'a guest who logs in later' 0 findings. 'can't' and 'cannot' became negations in #369 (F-0125), so these were silent before (from reading the code)
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/common/src/check/words.ts limitsOf/negates: NEGATION is searched in the whole clause before the limit, not only in the words that govern it
A test that would catch it: packages/common/tests/derivesQuotes.test.ts: readLimits("A guest who can't pay yet keeps at most 20 items") is [<=20], while readLimits('cannot exceed 20') stays [<=20]
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

A negation or refusal in a clause of its own before the limit (who, that, when, … with the limit's own verb after it) no longer turns the limit round.
