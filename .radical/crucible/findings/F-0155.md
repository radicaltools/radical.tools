## Decision (2026-11-15)

A minus before a percentage meaning a discount ("-10%"), and "minus X" meaning a subtraction, are no sign of the value: a requirement writing "a discount of 10%" or "subtract 5 €" passes. The sign stays for temperatures, balances and the like. To be written into the model's requirement on the branch that fixes it.

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

A minus before a percentage (a discount) and "minus" after a value or a noun (a subtraction) are no sign of the value; after a verb, a preposition or a limit the sign stays.
