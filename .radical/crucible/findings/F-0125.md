## Steps

1. Need 'Brief': 'Q: What did you say?\nA: A basket holds at most 20 items.'
2. Requirement (ubiquitous) action 'ensure that a basket cannot exceed 20 items', derives from the need with quote 'A basket holds at most 20 items.'
3. radical check (or read the add_relation answer of the MCP server)

## What differs

```text
Expected: No quote-value-differs: 'cannot exceed 20' allows up to and with 20, as 'at most 20' does
Actual: quote-value-differs 'allows above 20 ("exceed 20") where the words it quotes allow up to and with 20' for 4 of 4 phrasings (cannot exceed, can't exceed, doesn't exceed, cannot hold more than); control 'holds at most 20' gets none
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/common/src/check/words.ts limitsOf: negation regex /\b(?:not|never|isn't|aren't|neither|nor)\b/ misses cannot and n't contractions (can't, doesn't, won't, mustn't, shouldn't, don't)
A test that would catch it: packages/common/tests/derivesQuotes.test.ts: readLimits('cannot exceed 20') and readLimits("can't exceed 20") are [<=20]; a requirement 'cannot exceed 20 items' quoting 'at most 20 items' has no quote-value-differs
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Negation covers cannot, the n't forms and "no" right before a limit, and a limit inside one already read replaces the not-lookbehind, so "must not exceed 20" reads as ≤20.
