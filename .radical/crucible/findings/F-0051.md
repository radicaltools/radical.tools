## Steps

1. Open the sample, Designer, Payment Process
2. View properties → Viewpoint → State machine · Structure
3. Reload

## What differs

```text
Expected: The group opens on its first view (as New view and Quick Search do since F-0021), never as a canvas of its own; perhaps asked before the 15 picked elements are dropped
Actual: No question; route stays /v/view-payment-process (the group), 27 elements drawn on it and 29 positions saved into the group; its 15 picked elements become 0; a second 'Payment lifecycle' view is made; after a reload the route is a member view with 7 drawn. The same via the metamodel editor (View per element → Domain on the Domain model viewpoint while Domain model is open: 8 drawn on the group, 2 after a reload; p35-mm-subject.mjs)
Seen: 2 of 2 (+1 by the metamodel editor)
Where it probably comes from: guess: packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts applyViewpoint (and the metamodel editor's viewpoint change) let _sync() make the group but never move activeViewId to firstViewOfGroup; the F-0021 fix was in navigation only
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests: after applyViewpoint(active view, a viewpoint with subject), or a viewpoint gaining a subject, activeViewId is the group's first view and the group has no positions; e2e: the route after a reload is unchanged
```

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

A view that becomes a group of views per element (by its viewpoint, or by its viewpoint getting a subject) now opens the group's first view: the group keeps no positions or camera, a reload shows the same view, and View properties asks first when the view's hand-picked elements would go.
