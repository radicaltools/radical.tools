## Steps

1. Open the sample, view Core Banking
2. View properties: Lines = Orthogonal

## What differs

```text
Expected: the relation is routed around Payments Service and its components
Actual: the direct Z: (1488,782) -> x=1110 -> (732,667), through Payment Validator (952..1152 x 762..882) and FX Engine (952..1152 x 562..682). Offline, the router finds no route because the end's own padded keep-out block walls in the exit cell, depending on grid alignment set by far-away obstacles (without the Risk & Data group it routes); Authorization orthogonal: 13 of 17 needed routes fail, 9 succeed with the keep-out 10 px further back
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/layout/src/edgeRouting.ts routeAround/keepOut (keep-out padded by OBS_PAD = EXIT_DIST walls in the exit cell on some grid offsets) -> computeRoutedEdge falls back to plain()
A test that would catch it: packages/layout/tests/edgeRouting.test.ts: computeRoutedEdge with these 9 obstacles from (1487.9,782.4,left) to (732.1,667.4,right), orthogonal and curved, returns points clear of every obstacle, also with the grid origin shifted 0..14 px
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

The keep-out blocks behind a relation's ends are blocked unpadded in routeAround, so they no longer wall in the exit cell on some grid offsets; routes that found nothing on the sample's views dropped from 31 to 4 of 58.
