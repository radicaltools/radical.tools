## Steps

1. Open the sample model, Core Banking, in Designer
2. Drag Access & Security by its header 20 px right, 10 px down (it is pinned)
3. Double-click Payment Validator → Tidy around

## What differs

```text
Expected: Only Payment Validator and what it relates to move; Access & Security (pinned, unrelated) keeps its place and size
Actual: Access & Security (pinned, pin kept) goes from (1488, 453) 520x568 to (1515, 391) 360x630 while its children Auth Service and API Gateway stay; unrelated Risk & Data goes +28, -62, 658x498 -> 630x560; Payments Service +36, -64. After #367 this also happens with no move near the selection: after a Smart Layout, Hide Transactions DB, then Tidy around Auth Service says '1 move' (Risk & Data fitted round what the view shows)
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/layout/src/tidy/layout.ts Layout constructor: fitAll() refits every frame, the fixed ones (pinned, holdsFixed, and everything outside the selection in Tidy around) included; tidy/index.ts tidyOnce hands them over and, since #367, as a 'fit' step even with no move (guess). The sample's frames hold their children about 58 px from the top, while Tidy's compoundPadding wants 120
A test that would catch it: packages/layout/tests/tidy/frames.test.ts: runTidyLayout with around: [X] and pinned: [F], where F is a frame whose children sit 58 px below its top and has no relation with X: F's position and size are as given, and no 'fit' step names F or any frame outside X's reach
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Tidy no longer fits pinned or out-of-reach frames, gives back frames where nothing in them moved, and the store and MCP refit keep what Tidy kept (keptFrames).
