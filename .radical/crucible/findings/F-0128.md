## Steps

1. Commit: need with answers '…at most 20 items' and '…kept for 30 days'; 'Basket limit' (20 items) quoting the first; 'Idle baskets' (delete after 7 days) deriving from nothing
2. update_relation: quote of 'Basket limit' -> 'An idle basket is kept for 30 days.'
3. add_relation: 'Idle baskets' derives from the need quoting 'An idle basket is kept for 30 days.'
4. get_changes since HEAD; review_requirements since HEAD

## What differs

```text
Expected: Both requirements reviewed next to their new quotes
Actual: get_changes: Relations 1 added, 1 changed; review_requirements: 'Nothing to review: no requirement among the requirements added or changed since HEAD' (0 of 2 reviewed)
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/mcp/src/folderModel.ts reviewRequirements: `changed` is built from report.elements.added/changed only; derives relations added or changed are not counted for their source requirement
A test that would catch it: apps/mcp/src/folderModel.test.ts: after a commit, changing only a derives relation's quote (or adding a derives between existing elements) makes review_requirements list the source requirement
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

Review_requirements counts a requirement whose derives relation was added, changed or removed since the base as changed.
