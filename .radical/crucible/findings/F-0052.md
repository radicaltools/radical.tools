## Steps

1. Open the sample
2. Metamodel editor → List → Viewpoints → Delete viewpoint State machine
3. Close the editor and look at the list of views

## What differs

```text
Expected: Unclear: either the views stay as plain views with their layouts, as the button's title 'Delete viewpoint; its views keep their kind and filter' says, or Studio asks first and the texts say views of a group go
Actual: No question, no notice; views 36 → 34: Payment lifecycle (10 positions) and Fraud case lifecycle (8) are deleted, State machines becomes a structure view of 27 elements. MCP delete_viewpoint does the same and reports only 'Changed: metamodel.json, views.json' (p29-mcp-delvp.mjs). The scenario 'A group of views of one element' says the views go when the group is detached
Seen: 2 of 2 (Studio), 1 of 1 (MCP)
Where it probably comes from: packages/common/src/model.ts syncViewpoints drops the viewpoint, then planViewGroups removes members of what is no longer a group; MetamodelEditor.tsx Delete viewpoint title
A test that would catch it: packages/common/tests/viewpoints.test.ts: deleting a viewpoint with a subject either keeps its member views (no partOf, positions kept) or the delete reports the views it removes; e2e on the metamodel editor
```

## Decision (2026-10-23)

The user chose: deleting a viewpoint whose views are a group (a view per element) asks first and lists the views that will go; `delete_viewpoint` over MCP names the views it removed in its reply. The button's title and the tool's description are corrected to say so. The model's text is updated on the fix branch, with the code.

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

Deleting a viewpoint whose views are a group, or taking away what it draws a view per element of, now asks first in the metamodel editor and lists the views that go (Undo brings them back); delete_viewpoint and upsert_viewpoint name the deleted views in their reply, and the button's title and the tools' descriptions say so.
