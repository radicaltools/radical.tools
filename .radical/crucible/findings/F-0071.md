1. Sample, Core Banking: collapse Risk & Data
2. Select Event Bus, hold Alt, drag onto Risk & Data's 'inside' pill, release on the pill

## What differs

```text
Expected: Event Bus moves into Risk & Data with 'Moved 1 node into Group.', as with the group open (mode open)
Actual: Under the pill's centre is path.react-flow__edge-interaction of rf__edge-virtual-ctn-evtbus→dom-risk (z 1047); Event Bus stays in dom-banking, 0 notices
Seen: 3 of 3 (open control nests every time)
Where it probably comes from: guess: pills drawn under elevated/virtual edges; packages/ui/src/components/Canvas.tsx onMouseUp: a drop on an edge is neither field, node nor canvas, so nothing happens
```

The pills are drawn over the element in the viewport, above every line; lines take no pointer events during the gesture; and the drops that did nothing now say why.
