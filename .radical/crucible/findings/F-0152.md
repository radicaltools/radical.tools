## Steps

1. Need answer 'A password has between 8 and 64 characters.'
2. Requirement 'accept a password of at most 8 characters', derives quoting the answer; radical check
3. Control: answer 'A password has from 8 to 64 characters.'

## What differs

```text
Expected: quote-value-differs for 'at most 8' against 'between 8 and 64', as for 'from 8 to 64'
Actual: between … and: only quote-value-missing "64"; from … to: quote-value-missing and quote-value-differs 'allows up to and with 8 … where the words allow from and with 8'
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/common/src/check/words.ts limitsOf: clauses are split on \s(?:and|but|…)\s before RANGE runs, so 'between 8' and '64 characters' are read apart
A test that would catch it: packages/common/tests/derivesQuotes.test.ts: readLimits('between 8 and 64 characters') is [>=8, <=64]
```

## Fixed (2026-11-15)

A sentence is no longer cut into clauses at the "and" of "between N and M" or at a comma inside a number, so the range (and 1,000) is read whole.
