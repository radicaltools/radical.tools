label: "Fitness functions classified as the book does"
alternatives: "A sensor element in the model with run, when and labels, measuring fitness functions: a sensor is execution, not architecture, and would put commands and environments into the model. Sensors declared in a sensors.json beside check.json: invisible in Studio and the wiki, and the fitness function would not say how it is checked. A free command line as the measure: anything runs and nothing can be checked. Structured measure fields (sensor, args, env): heavier in Markdown and one measurement per function. Nightly as temporal: the book means a deadline (check again in six months) by temporal, not a schedule. An owner, a status (proposed, active, retired) and proactivity (intentional, emergent): left out by the user. Reviews in the measurement store, as a pull request label, or as a CI artifact: out of git they do not reach CI, a label ties the model to GitHub and the gate, an artifact expires."
consequences: "Adding a fitness function with a sensor radical sense has is a change to the model alone: the night or the gate runs it, and the review of the pull request sees what will run. A new kind of sensor is an adapter in the CLI; the workflows stay as they are, but for what the sensor needs (a browser, a secret), which is the worker's. The model runs commands in CI through its measure lines, with the trust of the repository it is in; a federated model's are never run. Every concern pack's fitness functions, Forge's prompts, the wizard, the card, the table of fitness functions and the wiki move from category to the classification; older models and packs read the same. A review is a commit by the person who made it."
context: "A fitness function had a description, a category (structural, operational, process or holistic) and its metrics. The category mixed what the function protects with how much of the system it takes in (holistic is a scope, not a kind of characteristic), and nothing said when it runs, whether a person runs it, or what to run: the nightly workflow named the test, its parameters, the mapping of its output to metrics and the metrics that were the night's (--expect studio.). A fitness function added to the model was run by nothing until someone changed the workflow. Building Evolutionary Architectures (Ford, Parsons, Kua, Sadalage) classifies fitness functions by scope (atomic or holistic), cadence (triggered, continual or temporal), result (static or dynamic) and invocation (automated or manual), and lets architects define which, when and how often they run."
date: "2026-10-30"
decision: "A fitness function states what it protects and how it is checked, and the sensors that execute it stay outside the model (ADR Sensors measure, the model decides). It has a characteristic, one of ISO/IEC 25010:2023 (functional suitability, performance efficiency, compatibility, interaction capability, reliability, security, maintainability, flexibility, safety) or other; a scope, atomic or holistic; a cadence: triggered with the event it runs on (pull-request, nightly, weekly, release, deploy), continual (measured all the time, in production), or temporal with every (it falls due after a time, e.g. 90d); an invocation, automated or manual; and, automated, a measure: one line per measurement, a sensor of radical sense and its arguments (`playwright tests/studio/scale.spec.ts --cwd apps/e2e --env SCALE_COPIES=14 --label model=radical-x14`). Its result is read from its metrics, static for fixed limits and dynamic for one against a baseline, and shown, not stated. `radical sense run --trigger <event>` runs the measure of every automated fitness function triggered on the event; the nightly workflow and the pull-request gate run it and judge what they ran, and radical check --trigger pull-request leaves out what runs on another event. A manual one is reviewed by a person: `radical sense review` records the result in reviews.json in the model folder, kept in git as the one exception to measurements out of git, and its `review(<name>) passed` condition reads it, stale after max_age. A model saved with category is classified on read: holistic gives scope holistic, structural the characteristic maintainability, and one checked by tests alone runs triggered on pull requests, automated; the rest stays empty, and fitness-fn-unclassified says what each lacks."
