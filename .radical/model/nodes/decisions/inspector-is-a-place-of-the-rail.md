label: "Inspector is a place of the rail"
alternatives: "Keep the perspective and give it a read-only switch: a second switch saying what the perspective already says. The button in the toolbar beside the perspectives, then in a status bar along the window's foot: the product owner moved it to the rail, beside Settings. A panel under the view, its height dragged, then a panel sliding up over the left panel and the view: the product owner wanted it to work as the Views section does, a submenu and the data in place of the canvas. Check settings as a tab of the Inspector: they are the repository's settings of the gate, not a result of the check."
consequences: "The report has the view's room, and the view is one click away without leaving the perspective; the left panel and the rail work for the Inspector as they work for the views. Studio has four perspectives again; the route names the Inspector's tab and verification path while it is open, instead of an inspector mode. The store loses the appMode 'inspector' and the parking it needed to show the live model beside a milestone; while a milestone is shown, the fixes wait for the live model. The metamodel editor keeps its own Validation list."
context: "The Inspector was a fifth perspective (Inspector perspective). Designer, Viewer and Presenter say what the user does with the model: edit it, look at it, present it. The Inspector says what state the model is in, which matters in each of them. As a perspective it took the whole window: every Open left the report for Designer, and the way back was Back. Its one-step fixes and its Settings changed the model and check.json from a perspective that looked read-only, so Viewer's promise that nothing changes by accident did not hold from there. The product owner: it is not a perspective (Inspector as a panel)."
date: "2026-11-15"
decision: "The Inspector becomes a place of the activity rail, as the Views section is, in Designer, Viewer and Presenter. Its button sits on the rail, just above Settings, drawn as the left panel's section tabs and coloured as the verdict, with the count of the check's errors under its icon (red, green with none). It, or ⌘J (Ctrl+J), shows the Inspector's tabs in the left panel in place of the sections, each a card as the Views section draws its views, and the tab picked in place of the view; lit, it folds the left panel and opens it again as a section's tab does, the report staying; a section's tab or ⌘J goes back to the view. The toolbar keeps only the perspectives. The Inspector stays open or closed across perspectives and reloads. What it may do follows the perspective it is open in: in Designer its one-step fixes edit the model; in Viewer and Presenter it edits nothing, and each fix reads '… in Designer', which shows Designer and makes the edit there. An element it names is shown in the perspective open, on the view Quick Search would pick, selected with its properties card. Its Settings tab (check.json: goals, rules, test files) leaves it for Inspector settings… in the Settings menu, beside the metamodel editor and the AI providers. A link to the old perspective opens the Inspector in Viewer."
