type: "requirement"
label: "Kept alignments"
action: "Studio and the MCP server shall keep the drawn centres of those elements on one horizontal (row) or vertical (column) line through drags, moves into another parent (at once, as a reload draws them), the live physics and Smart Layout, on that canvas only; when the alignment keeps its order, keep the elements along the line (left to right, top to bottom) in the order the user selected them in (for an agent, the order of nodeIds), moving the groups they sit in into that order when it runs across groups, and keep it across reloads; for a grid, keep every row and every column of cells filled row by row in that order; and take an element moved into another member of the alignment out of it; lining up shall move only what the line needs: a pinned element stays and the others come onto its line, unless the line's pinned members sit on different lines (then the rule wins), only what the members then cover is pushed aside, and only the containers round what moved are fitted, so a pinned container, or one the alignment has nothing to do with, keeps its place and size."
ears_type: "state-driven"
precondition: "a canvas has an alignment of two or more elements"
rationale: "Some arrangements carry meaning (a tier, a flow, a reading order) that no layout score knows about; the user states it once instead of fixing it after every layout. Keep in a row refitted every frame of the view and pushed aside pinned elements and groups the row had nothing to do with (F-0174, round 211–220).
