label: "Frames round their children"
action: "A frame that holds elements shall be sized to the children the view shows, with its padding on every side, never smaller than its collapsed size, in Studio and in Smart Layout alike; Smart Layout shall hand a frame back fitted so even when it has no move left to make."
ears_type: "ubiquitous"
rationale: "A frame kept at its empty default size leaves room round one or two children that reads as missing content. A view showing part of what a frame holds (a view per element of Entities of a domain) opens on All elements' places: the frame as wide as everything it holds there and the part shown at its far end, which a layout with nothing left to tidy used to keep."
