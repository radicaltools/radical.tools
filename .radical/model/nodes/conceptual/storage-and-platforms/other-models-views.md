type: "requirement"
label: "Other models' views"
action: "Studio and the MCP server shall list them in a view folder named after that model, with the folders they have there under it, each drawn as that model draws it, on every kind of view (canvas, table, wiki, Warnier, matrix, hierarchy), and with only that model's elements and those of the models it depends on, and shall refuse every change to them, saying which model they belong to and that a copy can be changed; the view folder of each such model and the folders under it shall be marked as that model's and read-only: no view of this model may be put in them (made there, moved there or dropped there), and they may not be renamed, moved or edited; another model's group of views per element shall stay that model's, its views listed under it, and no sync in Studio or over MCP (of groups, of viewpoints, Radical Forge's) shall make, rename, move or remove a view because of it; while such a view is shown, its read-only bar shall sit in a row of its own above the view, on every kind of view, covering none of the view's controls"
ears_type: "event-driven"
rationale: "A team reads another team's architecture as that team drew it, without leaving its own model. A folder per model keeps two views of one name apart (orders › Containers, billing › Containers). A view whose filter decides what it shows would otherwise also show the model's own elements; scoped to its model and that model's dependencies, it shows what it shows in its own repository. Changing it belongs in its repository; a change here would be lost at the next fetch. Evidence: packages/common/src/federation.ts (withOtherModels: views with their origin and scope, in a folder of their model), packages/common/src/viewPipeline.ts (viewShownNodes scoped), packages/common/src/model.ts (viewRefusal) A view of the model's own put in another model's folder would read as that model's, and is lost from sight when the folder goes with a fetch. Another model's group of views per element made views in this model's folder at each sync (F-0163), and the read-only bar lay over the wiki's Single page / Multi page, the hierarchy's Size by and the matrix's title (F-0165, round 211–220). Evidence also: packages/common/src/model.ts (planViewGroups, syncViewpoints leave views with an origin alone), apps/studio/src/renderer/src/App.tsx (other-model-shown)"
trigger: "a fetched model has views"
