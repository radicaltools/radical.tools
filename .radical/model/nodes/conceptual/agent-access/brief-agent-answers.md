In the Radical benchmark the agent with Radical was the only one short of context, and by its own tools: search_model's rows with every field and the whole description (up to 50,000 characters an answer) were a third of all it read, get_model_summary (35,000 characters at every call) another sixth. Asked one by one: search_model answers briefly by default and gives the whole element on request; get_model_summary names each type briefly and gives a type's whole definition on request.
