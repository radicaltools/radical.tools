label: "Sense of a quoted value kept"
action: "it shall report a warning on the derives relation naming what the requirement says and what the words say"
ears_type: "event-driven"
rationale: "Values that agree can still say opposite things: \"up to 90\" and \"refuses 90 or more\" hold the same number, and so do 30 days and 30 minutes. A range was cut into clauses at its \"and\" or at the comma of 1,000 (F-0152), a negation in a clause before the limit turned it round (F-0153), and 10 years against 10 days or 5 € against 5 USD went unseen (F-0154, round 211–220)."
trigger: "radical check or the Inspector checks a requirement whose quote puts a limit on a number (at most, up to, more than, below, from … to, between … and) that the requirement puts on the other side of that number (a refusal, and a negation, read as the other side: not, never, cannot, an n't form such as doesn't, or no right before the limit, unless they belong to a clause of their own before it, as in \"a guest who can't pay yet keeps at most 20\", so that \"must not exceed 20\" allows up to and with 20), says an amount of time (up to months and years) or money whose number the requirement gives in another unit or currency (5 € against 5 USD), lists in the order opposite to the quote's (oldest first, newest first, ascending, descending), or names a rounding the requirement does not"
