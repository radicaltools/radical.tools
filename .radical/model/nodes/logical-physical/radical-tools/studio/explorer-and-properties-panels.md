packages/ui/src/components/RightPanel.tsx (ActivityRail, LeftPanel and RightPanel), SelectionActions.tsx, ViewPipelinePanel, NodeWizard, EarsQuickEntry, RefPicker, propertiesCover.ts, store/showInView.ts, hooks/menuKeys.ts. Along the window's left edge the activity rail, as in VS Code: the left panel's sections Views, Milestones and Sequences as tabs at its top (each its icon, colour and count, on hover what it holds; the open one folds the panel away and back, another opens the panel on it), and at its foot the app's own actions, which Studio puts there (useRailSlot). Beside it the left panel shows the open section, a chevron on its edge folding it: All elements (the whole model as a table) first, view cards coloured by view type (while Changes on branch is on, each with the number of changed elements it shows), the active one scrolled into sight, + New view kept to the section's bottom edge (a blank table or one of the metamodel's viewpoints) and the properties of a view (name, folder, lines, viewpoint, view type — Structure, Flow, Hierarchy, Table, Matrix, Wiki or Warnier —, layout and its Filter: a chain of levels with their filters, the order; read-only with Detach for a view of a viewpoint, Save as viewpoint otherwise) or a milestone, each ending with its red Delete, sliding over the list. Over the right side of the view, the properties card of the one selected element, relation or sequence: it slides in on a selection and out without one, never resizing the view; its head holds the type, the name edited in place, where it sits, Wizard and Explore buttons and ✕; while Changes on branch is on, a section under it says the element is new or lists each changed field with the words removed and added since the base; its attributes are rows edited in place, long fields Markdown (EARS editor, wireframe, Hub template section); its foot, pinned to the card's bottom and shaded as its head is, holds the views that show the element (each opening that view with the element selected and in sight: showNodeInView), the element's or relation's actions (Wrap into…, Move to…, Unwrap, Align children…, Hide from view; Change source…, Change destination…) and Delete. While several elements are selected in Designer, the selection bar over the top of the canvas (SelectionActionBar) holds their count and actions. For an element or relation of another model of a federation the card is read-only, without the Wizard and Delete, and a note under its head (OtherModelNote) names the model and version. ElementCard is the same card for one given element outside the canvas, read-only, with actions under its head and the branch's changes given to it (Studio's Inspector, whose Views open in Designer). The node-creation wizard. The Elements palette is part of the canvas (ElementPalette). Another model's view of a federation sits in the view folder of its model with the model's name on its card; its properties are read-only with the note OtherModelViewNote, and the bar over it (OtherModelViewBar) says whose it is and offers Copy to this model. Another model's view folder, and every folder in it (otherModelFolder), is drawn with a dashed folder and the model's name on its top folder, offers nothing to edit, and is left out of the folder picker; the store refuses a view of the model's own going into it (viewFolderRefusal). The card's foot starts with Verification (components/ElementVerification.tsx), from what the host puts in store/verification.ts (useCardVerification: evidence and findings by element): the status, the conditions that are no test with their measurements, the elements measured for, Checked by, the findings with the elements they name as links, and Path for a need, requirement, scenario, ADR or fitness function when the host sets useVerificationPath (store/showInView.ts), greyed out with its reason while the host has no path to show. ElementCard takes onNavigate: where its links (CardNavigation) lead instead of the selection.
