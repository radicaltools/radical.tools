apps/studio/src/renderer/src: App.tsx (the layout grid: the activity rail, the left panel and the view, or the Inspector's tabs and report in their place), route.ts (the route names the place on screen in one form: document, perspective, view, the Inspector's tab and verification path while it is open, selected element, wiki page, milestone, slide; a change of place pushes it onto the window's history, applying one replaces), navHistory.ts (the routes pushed, with labels and times, for the toolbar), Toolbar (grouped as VS Code: the Radical logo, which opens About Radical Studio — components/AboutDialog.tsx and about.ts show the version, commit and day of the build, where Studio runs and the model folder format, with Copy and a link to the changelog —; Undo and Redo in one group in Designer; in every perspective Back and Forward just before Quick Search and History just after it, components/NavHistory.tsx, with Alt+← / Alt+→, ⌘[ / ⌘] and the desktop app's mouse side buttons; the perspective switch on the right (Designer, Viewer, Presenter); and, put on the activity rail, Models at its top (the model open named first, with where it is kept) and at its foot the Inspector's button and Settings (Metamodel editor, Inspector settings…, AI, Export): AppMenu, RailMenu, InspectorRailButton; leftPanelFold.ts keeps the left panel open or folded), Welcome screen, Quick Search, selection and edge action bars, PNG/SVG export and copy to clipboard (hooks/useExport.ts), components/BranchChanges.tsx (what the Inspector's This branch says of the base: BaseSince, SaveBaseHelp, the counts). Keeps the hash route (#/d/<doc>/m/<mode>/v/<view>/…) in step with the open document and view. Quick Search (components/QuickSearch.tsx) lists the hits of @radical/common's search in sections, the places visited last when nothing is typed, the suggestions of prefixes, and with > the commands that the toolbar, the rail and the find bar register (commands.ts); components/FindInView.tsx is the find bar over the view shown (Cmd/Ctrl+F). At the toolbar's left, OtherModelsButton (components/OtherModels.tsx) shows the other models of a federation from useOtherModelsStore (persistence/otherModels.ts, otherModelsSummary): their count or the problems, and a popover with each model's pin and fetched commit, the ones fetched through another, the fetch time, the warnings with radical federation fetch, and a click that reveals a model's folder of views.
