label: "State machines as nested states"
alternatives: "The full UML notation (regions, entry and exit points, junctions and forks as their own types): more types without more meaning for what people, agents and Forge write. A region type: a parallel state's children are regions already. A transition that points at its event node, or emits from a transition: a relation cannot start or end at a relation, so the link is by name. Events emitted only by the machine (the first draft): too coarse, the state that raises an event is what an implementation needs. A separate behaviour preset: models on the governance preset would not get it, and presets extend each other. Text-only events on transitions: no catalogue to link to the C4 elements that emit them."
consequences: "Every model on the unedited governance preset gets the types on its next load (ADR Edited presets fork). The metamodel diagram has a Behaviour frame; unit and e2e counts were raised to match. Renaming an event node leaves its transitions with an unknown-event warning until they are renamed too. Statecharts are laid out by the general Smart Layout, which knows nothing of their cycles and long labels yet; a layered layout for them and the Forge stage are the next steps."
context: "The product owner asked for event-driven hierarchical state machines in the metamodel, and for a Radical Forge stage that derives them from the scenarios before the mockups. The lifecycle of an entity (an order, a payment) had no notation: Gherkin scenarios describe its state changes, but nothing in the model held the states. The metamodel already has nesting (canvas containment), typed relations with properties and soft validation."
date: "2026-10-09"
decision: "The C4 + DDD + Governance preset gets four node types, state-machine, state, pseudostate and event, and three relation types, transition, lifecycle-of (machine → system, container, component, web app or domain) and emits (element → event), with SCXML / XState semantics. States nest by containment inside a machine or another state; a state with child states is compound; kind parallel makes its child states regions active at once; kind final ends its parent. A machine and every compound state that is not parallel enter through one initial pseudostate with a single transition to the default child. A transition carries event, guard, actions and kind (external or internal) as properties and names its event by the label of an event node, which @radical/common's statechart.ts checks with the other statechart rules as warnings in validateModel. An event is published by a state (emits with on: entry, exit or do, as SCXML raises in onentry and onexit), by a machine when the state is not known yet, or by a C4 element; a transition publishes one with `raise <Event>` (or send, emit) in its actions, and those names are checked too when quoted or capitalised. satisfies, verifies and illustrates also reach state machines and states. The canvas draws statechart notation, labels a transition `event [guard] / actions`, and the live layout nests machines and states like systems, domains and groups."
status: "accepted"
