label: "No relation to what holds it"
action: "Studio and the MCP server shall refuse it with a message saying that the one holds the other, and the canvas shall not mark the element that holds it as a target for such a type while connecting; and an element moved into another (nested by the special key, Move to…, the card's Parent, move_node) shall lose its structural relations with what then holds it, and so shall what it holds, keeping the governance ones, Studio warning which relations went and move_node saying so; and Propagate shall leave out of each later milestone and the live model the relations that model refuses so (one made in the milestone where one end holds the other there, or that of an element nested in the milestone with its new holder), with a notice naming them and where."
ears_type: "unwanted-behaviour"
rationale: "Containment already says how the two belong together; a relation between them repeats it and draws a line from an element to its own frame. A governance relation says what containment does not: a requirement kept in the system that satisfies it lost satisfies for good, and radical check then said nothing satisfied it (F-0035, round 177–184); the maintainer decided on 2026-10-23 that governance relations are outside this rule. A transition to a state that holds its source is SCXML (it leaves and enters that state again), so it stays allowed. Propagate carried a milestone's nest and relations into later models where one end held the other, which Studio drew nowhere and offered no way to remove (round 185–192, 2026-10-26, F-0070). Evidence: packages/common/src/model.ts (relationsRefusedHere); packages/common/src/relationRules.ts (relationRefusal, relationMayJoinHolder); packages/common/src/metamodel/types.ts (RelationTypeDef.governance); packages/common/src/viewPipeline.ts (connectTypes); apps/studio/src/renderer/src/components/MetamodelEditor.tsx (Governance)"
unwanted_condition: "a new relation, or a relation whose end is moved, would join an element and an element that holds it at any depth (a container and the system it is in) with a structural type: not a governance one (satisfies, constrains, verifies and the other types the metamodel marks governance), nor a transition of a state machine"
