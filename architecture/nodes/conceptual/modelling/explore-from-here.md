"Explore from here: we click a node and press Explore from here... a temporary view appears; we click + and its direct relations appear, once more and the next ones... We can save the view however we like."
Settled after questions: a + on every element of the view, adding that element's direct neighbours only (plus one button for a whole level); every relation counts, in either direction and of any type; a neighbour inside a container appears inside the frames of its ancestors; the temporary view stays at the top of the view list until it is closed or another exploration starts, and is then gone without a question, never in the document; Save as view keeps it as an ordinary view with a name and a folder.
Then: "I want it to work in Viewer too." Settled: in Viewer only exploring (no Save as view, Viewer saves nothing); it explores the model shown, a milestone's when one is shown.
Then: "Explore from here needs a special layout... more hierarchical, top-down?" Settled: layers by distance from the start element (not by relation direction), the whole exploration laid out again on each +, frames kept with layers inside them.
Translated from Polish.
