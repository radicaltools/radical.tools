type: "requirement"
label: "Table shows the view's tree"
action: "The table view shall show what the view's filter gives as a tree — the chain's levels or, without a chain, the elements' containment — one row per element, every row as high as the others, with its name, a guide line per level, its type beside the name and the rows under it folding away, and its summary: one line of inline Markdown its type's summary template makes of the data it has, rendered, starting with what matters and ending with detail (a requirement's who shall do what before its condition, a scenario's outcome before its when, a state or event where it leads when it has no description), the whole of it as plain text in the tooltip (a requirement's whole EARS sentence); a click on a row selects the element, a double-click on it shows its attributes in the properties card (bringing it back if ✕ hid it), and F2 or Enter on the selected row renames it in the row."
ears_type: "ubiquitous"
rationale: "The filter in the view's properties decides what the table lists and how; the table shows it with the one line about each element that matters for its type, which the metamodel defines per type from fields the element already has. A requirement's line starts with what it requires, so that a cut-off line loses its condition, not its meaning. Its fields are edited in the properties card, which a click on its row opens, and its wizard is a button there; a single click renaming the row got in the way of opening it. Rows are of one height, since a long table places them by one measured height. Its former tabs per type, columns per property, Relations tab and row buttons are gone. Evidence: packages/ui/src/components/TableView.tsx (structureRows, renderName, renderSummary, openProperties, useTableWindow), packages/common/src/nodeSummary.ts (nodeSummary, nodeSummaryDetail), packages/common/src/metamodel/ears.ts (earsSummaryParts), packages/common/tests/nodeSummary.test.ts, apps/e2e/tests/studio/views.spec.ts, apps/e2e/tests/studio/view-pipeline.spec.ts; manual#view-table"
