rationale: "One viewpoint per question a team keeps asking of the model, along the relations the metamodel has for it; groups keep nineteen of them findable. A structure viewpoint names types and draws every relation between them, since containment through groups (a container in a group inside a system) is not one level. Evidence: packages/common/src/metamodel/presets/viewpoints.ts, packages/common/src/viewpoints.ts (viewpointGroups), packages/ui/src/components/RightPanel.tsx (balancedColumns, New view menu, Viewpoint optgroups), apps/studio/src/renderer/src/components/MetamodelEditor.tsx (Group); manual#viewpoints"
