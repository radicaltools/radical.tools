type: "requirement"
label: "Properties card over the view"
action: "Studio shall slide a properties card over the right side of the view without resizing the view, holding on its head its type, its name as a title edited in place, where it sits (the elements it is in, or a relation's two ends, as links that select them), a Wizard button for a type with a wizard, an Explore button with its icon and name that explores from an element (also in Viewer), and ✕; each short attribute a row of name and value that reads as text and edits in place, empty ones shown as — and a required one with no value marked, each long field rendered as Markdown and edited on a click; a foot pinned to the card's bottom edge while the attributes scroll, holding the element's views, its actions and, always in sight, Delete as a red outline across the card (an element with what it contains, or a relation; none in Viewer), the head and the foot a shade off the content; keep the card open while the user selects one element or relation after another with a single click, showing the one selected, and hide it when nothing is selected or several elements are, ✕ hiding it until it is opened again; keep its width as dragged at its edge; and frame a selection on the canvas in the part the card leaves free."
ears_type: "event-driven"
rationale: "A single click only selects, so that picking, connecting and dragging leave the view as it is; a double-click asks for the details, and the card then stays and follows the selection (2026-10-23). A panel beside the canvas moved the canvas's centre whenever it opened or closed, and its tree of the view's elements repeated what the canvas and the table show. Attributes that read as text and edit in place serve both reading and editing. A ⋯ menu held only one or two actions (Explore, Delete), a click away for nothing: Explore is a button on the head as Wizard is, and what is done to the element sits in a foot that never scrolls away, Delete last and red (2026-10-22). Evidence: packages/ui/src/components/RightPanel.tsx (RightPanel, PropertiesContent, PanelHeader, ActionIcon, DeleteButton, DeleteSection, AttrRow, AttrValue, MarkdownField), packages/ui/src/components/propertiesCover.ts, packages/ui/src/components/Canvas.tsx (frameNodes, frameSelection, __rfFocusNode), packages/ui/src/index.css (.right-panel, .pp-*, .pp-foot, --pp-chrome-bg), apps/studio/src/renderer/src/studio.css (.app-layout), apps/hub/src/hub.css, apps/e2e/tests/studio/properties-card.spec.ts"
trigger: "the user double-clicks an element, a relation or a row (on a canvas, in a table, a Warnier diagram or a matrix, or a rectangle without children in a hierarchy), opens one from Quick Search, the Inspector or a wiki page's [properties], or selects a sequence"
