type: "requirement"
label: "Per-view positions and camera"
action: "Studio shall save the element positions and camera of the outgoing view and restore those saved for the incoming view; keep each view's camera, and All elements', in this browser for the document, outside the model files, so a reload opens each view where it was; keep only Designer's camera as a view's camera, Viewer and Presenter keeping none of their own and opening each view at Designer's camera; open a model opened on a view (from Models, or a Structurizr import) at that view's camera; end a presentation started from Designer on the view and camera it started from; and leave the cameras out of Undo and Redo, so a step undone on another view keeps the cameras of both views."
ears_type: "complex"
precondition: "Studio is in the Designer perspective"
rationale: "Each view keeps its own arrangement, so arranging one diagram never disturbs another. An Undo that went back to another view put back the camera of the view left (round 185–192, 2026-10-26, F-0063), and since a reload saves nothing (D15) a camera moved on its own was lost on reload (F-0084); the cameras are kept per viewer, not in the model, so moving one changes no model file (not while a presentation plays, nor for an exploration). A model opened on a view from Models opened at another view's camera and a reload moved it (F-0095), a slide clicked in Presenter changed the camera Designer opened the view at after a reload (F-0096), and Esc after F5 left the view at the last slide's camera (F-0097) (round 193–200, 2026-10-26); it was decided that Viewer and Presenter keep no camera of their own and open each view at Designer's. Evidence: packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts (_restoreHistoryEntry, loadDiagram); packages/ui/src/store/viewCameras.ts; packages/ui/tests/views.test.ts:113-178; manual#views; packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts (startPresentation, stopPresentation)"
trigger: "the user switches to another view"
