label: "Card lists the element's views"
action: "the properties card shall list, in a Views section with their number, every view that shows the element (what its filter's chain gives, else the elements it lists, or every element when it shows the whole model; a group of views per element through its views), in model order, each with its name and view type and the open one marked, say so when no view shows it, and on a click on one, the open one too, open that view, scrolled into sight in the list of views, with the element selected and in sight: its containers open and the camera on it on a canvas, its page from its top on a wiki, its row scrolled to (the rows over it unfolded) in a table, its line scrolled to in Warnier; from the Inspector's card in Designer"
ears_type: "event-driven"
rationale: "An element sits on several views; finding the others meant opening each view or searching. The views are counted the way the view list counts them (viewShownNodes), so the card and the list agree. A table of fewer than 200 rows, a wiki opened after another wiki and Warnier kept the scroll they had, so the element was often out of sight, and the view opened could be out of sight in a long list of views (2026-10-22); each ask now bumps useShowRequest, which the list views scroll on, and the list of views scrolls to the active view.
