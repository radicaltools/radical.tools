label: "View shows exactly its filter"

ears_type: "state-driven"
precondition: "a view's filter holds a chain of levels, or the view lists its elements"
rationale: "The filter is what the view is: Filter, Structure and the view used to count differently (Folder model in Studio: 15 in Filter, 17 on the canvas with the Conceptual and Modelling frames). Positions stay relative to containers, so a container the filter does not give stays on React Flow, invisible.
