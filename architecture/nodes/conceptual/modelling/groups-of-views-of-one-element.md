type: "requirement"
label: "Groups of views of one element"
action: "Studio and the MCP server shall make the view a group holding a view of each element of the viewpoint's types that is not external, each kept to its element and what lies inside it and named after it; add a view to the group as soon as such an element is added and remove it with the element; lay out each new canvas view of a group with Smart Layout (the MCP server as it makes it, Studio the first time it is opened in the designer without positions of its own, on the live model: opened first in a milestone, it is laid out once the milestone is left); mark such viewpoints View per element in New view and such views in the list of views, list the group as one card that shows or hides its views on a click, opening its first view when made; keep the group's views in its folder (moving one of them to a folder moves the group) and in step with its viewpoint; keep the group's viewpoint, view type and filter and its views' viewpoint, view type, filter, name and elements as the viewpoint sets them (no View type, Viewpoint list, Detach, filter editing, renaming, picking or hiding an element there; over MCP such changes, set_view_nodes included, are refused); leave the group's views as they are when its element is renamed or deleted in a milestone, until that edit is propagated; delete them with the group; never keep a canvas for the group itself: a view that becomes a group (by its viewpoint, or by its viewpoint getting a subject) shall open the group's first view, keep no positions or camera of its own and show the same after a reload, View properties asking first, and update_view saying so, when the elements picked for the view would go; deleting the viewpoint of a group, or taking away the types it draws a view of each element of, shall delete the group's views, Studio asking first and listing them (Undo bringing them back), delete_viewpoint and upsert_viewpoint naming them in their reply; and the metamodel editor (View per element) and upsert_viewpoint (subjectTypes) shall let a viewpoint make a view of each element of chosen types."
ears_type: "event-driven"
rationale: "A state machine, a process, a system's containers and a container's components read on their own, as C4 draws them, and the list of views stays one entry per viewpoint however many there are; a new container shows up in its group without anyone making a view. Exploratory runs 28, 32 and 34 (2026-10-20) found an agent could pick a group view's elements, and that edits and openings in a milestone changed the live views. In run 183 (round 177–184, 2026-10-23) a view that became a group stayed a canvas of its own, with the positions and camera it had (F-0051), and deleting a viewpoint whose views were a group deleted them without a word (F-0052); the maintainer decided on 2026-10-23 that such a delete asks first and lists the views, and that delete_viewpoint names them. Evidence: packages/common/src/model.ts (isViewGroup, planViewGroups, syncViewGroups, groupViewNodesRefusal, renameViewsOf, forgetNodesInViews, applyViewpoint, deleteNode, groupLosses), packages/common/src/viewpoints.ts (scopedChain, subjectViewName), packages/common/src/ai/modelFacade.ts, packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts (_sync, _syncViewpoints, setActiveView, keepingSharedViews, arrangeOnFirstOpen, setViewNodes), packages/ui/src/components/RightPanel.tsx (group card, Group note), packages/common/src/ai/tools/viewTools.ts, packages/common/src/ai/tools/metamodelTools.ts, apps/studio/src/renderer/src/components/MetamodelEditor.tsx"
trigger: "the user or an agent makes a view of a viewpoint that draws one element (New view, the Viewpoint list, create_view or update_view)"
