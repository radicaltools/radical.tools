type: "requirement"
label: "Only what the view shows is offered"
action: "Studio shall offer to add only the element types some level of the view's chain can show (one its ways take and its filters do not leave out): on a canvas it shall grey out the other types in the Elements palette, not let them be dragged, and say in their tooltip that the view's filter does not show them; in a wiki it shall leave them out of Add element and Add child, and under an element's page offer to write what Add or link… offers there, the types of the chain's next level, linking what is written the way that level takes (on a requirement's page Scenarios and Mockups); at the top of the wiki (Add element, and the types offered to start with) it shall offer only the types of the chain's first level, placed where that level puts them, so that an element of a later level is added under an element of the level above it, offering them also where that level's filters by name or relation would not show a new one, marked \"the filter won't show it\" as the table does, and never say that the metamodel has no root types while the chain has some."
ears_type: "state-driven"
precondition: "a canvas or wiki view with a filter chain is shown"
rationale: "A view's filter decides what it shows; an element added there that it does not show was added out of sight, with only a warning after the fact. The palette and the wiki say beforehand what belongs on the view. An element of a later level shows only under one of the level above (a requirement under the need it derives from), so at the top it would be added out of sight too: the top offers what the table's + at the top offers. In run 180 (round 177–184, 2026-10-23) a requirement's page in a Specification wiki offered Requirements, which the view then hid, and never Scenarios or Mockups (F-0042), and a wiki whose first level filtered by name or relation said \"No root types in metamodel\" (F-0043). Evidence: packages/common/src/viewPipeline.ts (chainTypes, addChoices); packages/ui/src/components/ElementPalette.tsx; packages/ui/src/components/WikiView.tsx (childChoicesOf, topChoices, childTypesOf)"
