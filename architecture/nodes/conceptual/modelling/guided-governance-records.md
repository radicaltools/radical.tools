When I create an ADR or a requirement I do not want a wall of empty fields. Walk me through it step by step with a prompt for each part, end with the links that give it meaning, and do not leave a half-filled element behind if I give up. Our own types should be able to have such a guide too.

When I record a decision, a stakeholder need or a requirement I want to be walked through what matters, including the links to the parts of the architecture it governs, so the record is not just a title. Raw input such as a brief or meeting notes should be kept word for word, and I want to write requirements as plain EARS sentences rather than fill in a form.
