type: "requirement"
label: "Selection brings relations forward"
action: "the canvas shall draw the selection, the relations from and to it or to anything inside it, the elements at their other ends and the containers they sit in as they are, draw those relations in the accent colour and dim every other element, relation and relation label the canvas shows until the selection is cleared; when the selection was made by a plain click on the canvas and is not already wholly in view at a zoom that shows relation labels, the camera shall frame the selection with the elements at the other ends in the part of the canvas the palette leaves free, keeping room for the properties card only while it is open or asked for, zooming in to at most 100 % or the zoom in use when closer, and a click on the empty canvas shall bring back the camera from before unless the user moved it since, also while the framing is still on its way."
ears_type: "event-driven"
rationale: "On a large view the relations of one element are lost among all the others; the metamodel diagram already focuses and frames a selected type this way. The focus is computed once per change of the selection or the canvas; dimmed cards get a CSS class on an object kept per node, so no card re-renders, and opacity only (no filter) keeps panning fast. Only clicks on the canvas move the camera: Quick Search, a view switch or a slide that selects set their own, and a presentation never frames. A click on the empty canvas during the framing flight left the camera half-way (run 179, 2026-10-23, F-0041): the flight now counts as the framed camera, and only a camera move by the user keeps the camera where it is. Since a click only selects and the card opens on a double-click, the framing still kept room for a closed card: an empty band on the right, and at 1280 px an element under the palette (round 185–192, 2026-10-26, F-0061). Evidence: packages/ui/src/components/propertiesCover.ts (propertiesPanelCover), packages/ui/src/store/canvasFocus.ts, packages/ui/src/components/Canvas.tsx (focusDimmed, frameSelection, frameNodes, stillFramed), packages/ui/src/components/edges/RelationEdge.tsx (edgeFocus), packages/ui/src/index.css (.focus-dim), packages/ui/tests/canvasFocus.test.ts, apps/e2e/tests/studio/selection-focus.spec.ts"
trigger: "the user selects one or more elements, or a relation, on a canvas while Focus is on"
