label: "Layout quality ranking"
action: "Smart Layout shall choose among the candidates, their annealed and relaxed forms and the layout on screen by their aggregated quality: each criterion (crossings, crossings under 30°, relations leaving an element less than 10° apart, spread of edge lengths, relations drawn through elements, relations squeezed through one narrow place, compactness, aspect ratio, room for labels, labels left covering others or elements once placed, relations on one side of an element past four, and how unevenly the elements of a container fill its quadrants) measured on the relations as the canvas draws them, standardised over the layouts compared with a smallest meaningful difference per criterion, and summed with equal weights; it shall never choose a layout with overlapping elements while one without exists, and keep the layout on screen unless another is better by a quarter of a standard deviation in total."
ears_type: "ubiquitous"
rationale: "A weighted sum did not hold at every size: on radical.tools' own model the crossings outweighed everything else, and a sprawling and a tight layout scored within 3%. Aggregating standardised criteria follows Huang, Huang & Lin, Evaluating overall quality of graph visualizations based on aesthetics aggregation, Information Sciences 330 (2016) 444-454, with counts instead of their worst-angle measures. The score draws the relations as the canvas does (spread ports, routes around elements, rolled-up relations with all their names); equal weights do not yet match the author's judgement on the \"Shared packages\" view, see docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md (calibration). Evidence: packages/layout/src/ranking.ts, packages/layout/src/smartLayout.ts (renderEdges, scoreParts, labelCollisions, spaceImbalanceOf), packages/layout/src/scoreWeights.ts, packages/layout/tests/ranking.test.ts"
