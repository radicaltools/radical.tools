apps/web/index.html and landing.css: the hero shows the product as one picture, a map. The Radical Model sits in the middle (its rules, elements, views, history and files), with a coloured branch and arrowheads to each part: Radical Studio and Radical MCP read and write it, Radical Hub packs concerns in, Radical CLI checks it, Radical Forge generates it, and it harnesses your code. What is yours stands dashed at the edges and uses the parts: your agent uses Radical MCP, which uses Forge, and writes your code; your CI, between the CLI and your code, runs the CLI and tests the code. Every box is a link; on a narrow screen the same map is one column. Under it, Open Studio in the browser and Explore the Hub, then Made for: architects, vibe coders, business analysts, tech leads, teams cutting the cost of AI and companies with AI to govern, each with its first link. Loads theme.js before any stylesheet.
