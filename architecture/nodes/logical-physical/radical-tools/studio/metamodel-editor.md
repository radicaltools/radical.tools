MetamodelEditor.tsx and components/metamodel/ (MetamodelDiagram, MetamodelInspector, metamodelGraph): node and relation types with their properties, descriptions and wiki reading phrases, viewpoints (name, view type, description, the node types it makes a view of each of (View per element), the chain in words, how many views are of each), the Validation list, Reset to C4 preset, and a Diagram tab that draws the type graph.
