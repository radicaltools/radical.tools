packages/ui/src/components/WarnierView.tsx. Draws a view of kind warnier as a Warnier–Orr diagram from @radical/common/warnier (warnierTree): columns left to right, each element with a brace around what is under it, a step's repetition and condition under its name, ⊕ between alternatives and + beside concurrent parts. A click selects the element and opens its properties card.
