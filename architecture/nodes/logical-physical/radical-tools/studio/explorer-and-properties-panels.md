packages/ui/src/components/RightPanel.tsx (LeftPanel and RightPanel), SelectionActions.tsx, ViewPipelinePanel, NodeWizard, EarsQuickEntry, RefPicker, propertiesCover.ts, store/showInView.ts, hooks/menuKeys.ts. On the left an accordion of Views, Milestones and Sequences — each with its icon, colour and count, a folded one saying what it holds, the open one taking the free height — with All elements (the whole model as a table) first, view cards coloured by view type (while Changes on branch is on, each with the number of changed elements it shows), + New view (a blank table or one of the metamodel's viewpoints) and the properties of a view (name, folder, lines, viewpoint, view type — Structure, Flow, Hierarchy, Table, Matrix, Wiki or Warnier —, layout and its Filter: a chain of levels with their filters, the order; read-only with Detach for a view of a viewpoint, Save as viewpoint otherwise) or a milestone, each ending with its red Delete. Over the right side of the view, the properties card of the one selected element, relation or sequence: it slides in on a selection and out without one, never resizing the view; its head holds the type, the name edited in place, where it sits, Wizard and Explore buttons and ✕; while Changes on branch is on, a section under it says the element is new or lists each changed field with the words removed and added since the base; its attributes are rows edited in place, long fields Markdown (EARS editor, wireframe, Hub template section); its foot, pinned to the card's bottom and shaded as its head is, holds the views that show the element (each opening that view with the element selected and in sight: showNodeInView), the element's or relation's actions (Wrap into…, Move to…, Unwrap, Align children…, Hide from view; Change source…, Change destination…) and Delete. While several elements are selected in Designer, the selection bar over the top of the canvas (SelectionActionBar) holds their count and actions. ElementCard is the same card for one given element outside the canvas, read-only, with actions under its head and the branch's changes given to it (Studio's Inspector, whose Views open in Designer). The node-creation wizard. The Elements palette is part of the canvas (ElementPalette).
