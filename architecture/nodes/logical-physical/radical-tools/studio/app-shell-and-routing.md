apps/studio/src/renderer/src: App.tsx (the layout grid: the activity rail, the left panel and the view), route.ts (the route names the place on screen in one form: document, perspective, view, Inspector tab, selected element or the Inspector's open card, wiki page, milestone, slide; a change of place pushes it onto the window's history, applying one replaces), navHistory.ts (the routes pushed, with labels and times, for the toolbar), Toolbar (grouped as VS Code: the Radical logo, which opens About Radical Studio — components/AboutDialog.tsx and about.ts show the version, commit and day of the build, where Studio runs and the model folder format, with Copy and a link to the changelog —; Undo and Redo in one group in Designer; in every perspective Back and Forward just before Quick Search and History just after it, components/NavHistory.tsx, with Alt+← / Alt+→, ⌘[ / ⌘] and the desktop app's mouse side buttons; on the right Changes on branch in every perspective but the Inspector, beside the perspective switch; and, put at the foot of the activity rail, the menus Models, Metamodel editor, AI, Export and Settings that the Radical menu held: AppMenu, RailMenu), Welcome screen, Quick Search, selection and edge action bars, PNG/SVG export and copy to clipboard (hooks/useExport.ts), components/BranchChanges.tsx (the Changes on branch toggle: a small button with an icon and the number of changed elements, marked when the base is old, its name and what the count is about in a tooltip on hover, and, without a base, the commands that save one). Keeps the hash route (#/d/<doc>/m/<mode>/v/<view>/…) in step with the open document and view.
