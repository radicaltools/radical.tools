## Steps

1. Metamodel editor: untick every allowed parent and At the root of a type with no elements
2. Look at Validation and get_issues

## What differs

```text
Expected: Unclear: Validation lists what the model holds; a type nothing can be placed as is a metamodel issue of its own
Actual: No question, Validation and get_issues silent; the palette still offers the type
Seen: 2 of 2
```

## Decided

2026-10-23, the maintainer: the metamodel editor asks before it takes a type's
last place (its last allowed parent, or At the root when it has no parent),
naming that nothing could then be placed as that type, as narrowing the
metamodel asks; Validation and get_issues warn about a type nothing can be
placed as, also while no element of it exists.
