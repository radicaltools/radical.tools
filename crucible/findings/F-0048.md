## Steps

1. Open the sample, Domain model (entities; their domains are not drawn)
2. Calm drag Payment 500 px left and 300 px down onto empty canvas

## What differs

```text
Expected: Only Payment moves
Actual: Account x 245→−177 and Ledger Entry x −6→−428 (both 190 px from the drop, not covered; Ledger Entry ends under the palette), Fraud Case y −1.6; each is in another domain the view does not draw
Seen: 3 of 3
Where it probably comes from: guess: packages/layout/src/liveColaEngine.ts clearOverlaps: the undrawn Payments domain grows round the dropped entity and pushes the undrawn Accounts domain with its children
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests/calmDrag.test.ts or apps/e2e/tests/studio/physics.spec.ts: on view-domain-model drag Payment to empty canvas; no other drawn element moves by more than 1 px
```

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

A view's frames (undrawn containers) take no part in the live layout or the drop's pushes, and a frame that grows on placement makes no room, so a drop moves only what it covers.
