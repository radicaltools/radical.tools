## Steps

1. Open the bookstore fixture, Containers view, Designer
2. Click Smart Layout (8 moves)
3. Drag Payment Provider down 150 px and back up 150 px (pinned, same place)
4. Click Smart Layout: 'nothing left to tidy. Unpinned 1 element.'
5. Press Ctrl+Z

## What differs

```text
Expected: Ctrl+Z gives the pin back and nothing moves
Actual: Ctrl+Z undoes the second drag: Payment Provider y 169.6 -> 505.2; the pin comes back only with the drag's step. The unpin itself had no undo step
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: guess: packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts runSmartLayout, the result.keptCurrent branch calls unpinLaidOut without _pushUndo()
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio: tidy a view, drag an element away and back, Smart Layout, Ctrl+Z: the element has not moved and is pinned again
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

The keptCurrent branch of runSmartLayout records an undo step before it unpins, so Undo gives the pin back and nothing moves.
