## Steps

1. Open f3-studio-view.radical (the 'Studio' view of the repository's own model, 30 drawn) in Designer
2. Select Canvas, Diagram store, Inspector, Metamodel editor, Wiki and table views → Align… → Keep in a column
3. Smart Layout six times

## What differs

```text
Expected: As without the column: from run 2 on, 'nothing left to tidy'
Actual: Run 1: 44 moves; then 2, 1, 3, 2, 1 moves in a cycle of three, each run moving 28–29 of 30 elements. Without the column: run 2 onwards 'nothing left to tidy'. On the injury Conceptual view a grid or a column of 6 mockups kept moving for 7 runs
Seen: 2 of 2 on the fixture, 1 of 1 on the full architecture copy
Where it probably comes from: packages/layout/src/tidy/index.ts runTidyLayout: what is handed over passes enforceAlignments and the top-left normalisation after the last judgement (guess); sibling of F-0081
A test that would catch it: packages/layout/tests/tidy/craft.test.ts: a view with a column of five siblings among related elements tidied three times: the second and third runs have no steps
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

With a row, column or grid set, Tidy hands over exactly the layout it judged instead of enforceAlignments' padded refit, so later runs find nothing.
