2. click Core Banking Platform
3. Presenter, F5

## What differs

```text
Expected: slide 1 drawn as captured: 0 elements dimmed, none selected (as when nothing was selected before)
Actual: 3 of 8 elements dimmed, sys-core drawn selected; the address while playing is .../e/sys-core/p/pres-walkthrough/play/1/sl/0, and that deep link opened fresh plays it dimmed too
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: guess: packages/ui/src/store/canvasFocus.ts selectCanvasFocus / Canvas.tsx focusDimmed apply the selection focus while presentationActive (frameSelection already skips presentations)
```
