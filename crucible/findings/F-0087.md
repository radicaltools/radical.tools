## Steps

1. Bookstore fixture, Models → Manage models → Move to a folder…
2. Select Payment Provider on System Context, press Shift+F10 (Discuss menu, focus on Discuss)
3. Press F5, then →, then Space, then Escape

## What differs

```text
Expected: The presentation starts without the menu (the canvas offers no Discuss menu while presenting); → goes to slide 2/2 as it does with no menu open; Space goes to the next slide
Actual: After F5 the Discuss menu is still drawn on the playing slide; → leaves it at 'Context 1 / 2' (control without the menu: 'Containers 2 / 2'); Space presses Discuss, and after Escape a discussion panel opens (1 panel). Same in Designer and Viewer
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/components/Canvas.tsx: discussionMenu is not cleared when presentationActive turns on; the menu's keepKeysHere stops arrows/Space before PresenterHUD's window listener (guess: clear it in an effect on presentationActive)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/discussions.spec.ts: open the Discuss menu with Shift+F10, press F5, expect no menuitem 'Discuss' visible, press →, expect the HUD to read 2 / 2
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

The canvas closes its Discuss menu, and the store the relation-type and Explore menus, when a presentation starts, so → and Space move the slides.
