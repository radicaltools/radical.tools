## Steps

1. Sample, Authorization view, Fit All (zoom 0.23)
2. Click 'Break-glass use alerts', hold Alt, drag to the centre of 'Leavers lose access' (a target with one way: derives from), release
3. Or: with Alt held, click the centre of 'Leavers lose access'

## What differs

```text
Expected: Break-glass use alerts derives from Leavers lose access (the click adds Leavers lose access to the selection)
Actual: Under the target's centre is the 'derives from' pill of 'Unpermitted calls refused'; the relation made is 'Break-glass use alerts' derives 'Unpermitted calls refused'. The Alt-click leaves the selection [Break-glass use alerts]. Core Banking zoomed to 0.15: a drop on FX Engine's centre made ctn-evtbus -> comp-validator
Seen: 2 of 2 (drop), 2 of 2 (click)
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/components/Canvas.tsx ConnectPills: since #259 drawn in the viewport at z-index 100000 and kept at screen size (--canvas-unzoom, .connect-pills bottom:100% in index.css), so at low zoom they stand over the elements above them; onMouseUp takes the pill's data-connect-target
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/nesting.spec.ts: sample Authorization at Fit All, Break-glass use alerts selected, Alt held: elementFromPoint at each target's centre is that element or its own pill; a drop at Leavers lose access's centre relates to it
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

The special key's press, move, release and click look through another element's pill to the element under the pointer, and a pill keeps a drop only where it covers no other element.
