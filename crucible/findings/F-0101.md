## Steps

1. Bookstore, Element Table: F2 on Orders DB, rename to Order Store, Enter
2. Ctrl+z (undone)
3. Ctrl+Shift+Z as Windows/Linux sends it (key 'Z', shiftKey)
4. Ctrl+y to compare; Ctrl+Z with key 'Z' and no Shift (Caps Lock)

## What differs

```text
Expected: Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes and Ctrl+Z undoes whatever the letter's case
Actual: after Ctrl+Shift+Z the name stays 'Orders DB' with Redo enabled; Ctrl+y redoes; Ctrl+Z with key 'Z' leaves 'Order Store'
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/studio/src/renderer/src/components/Toolbar.tsx keyboard handler: e.key === 'z' (real Chrome on Windows/Linux sends key 'Z' for Ctrl+Shift+Z; the e2e suite sends 'z')
A test that would catch it: a keydown {key:'Z', ctrlKey:true, shiftKey:true} redoes and {key:'Z', ctrlKey:true} undoes
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

The undo and redo letter is compared in lower case, so Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes on Windows and Linux and Ctrl+Z undoes with Caps Lock on.
