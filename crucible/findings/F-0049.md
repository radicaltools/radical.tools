## Steps

1. Open the sample, Payment lifecycle (a view per element)
2. Calm drag the state Refused 300 px down inside the state machine (the machine grows)
3. Switch to Fraud case lifecycle and back, or reload

## What differs

```text
Expected: The canvas as the drop left it (Refused at 60,911)
Actual: All 9 elements drawn in a new arrangement: Refused (60,911)→(1633,338), machine x 30→259; after a reload several lines end beside the final states and the saved positions still hold the drag (canvas and file disagree until the next edit saves the new places); a view switch saves them (10 positions); the machine's frame creeps for 3 s after the reload. Fraud case lifecycle the same (7 elements). A drag that does not grow the machine, or one that grows a process on Payment Process, changes nothing
Seen: 4 of 4 (plus 1 of 1 on Fraud case lifecycle)
Where it probably comes from: guess: the live layout's rebuild on opening the view frees the machine's states (its domain is not drawn there) and the physics re-spreads them; not saved on a reload
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/state-machines.spec.ts: drag a state in view-payment-lifecycle 300 px down, switch views and back, reload: transforms unchanged and equal to the saved views[].positions
```

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

A canvas measures a container with nothing in it at the folded size it draws it, and nothing counts as astray inside a frame, so a grown state machine is no longer placed afresh and rearranged on the next opening.
