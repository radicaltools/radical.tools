## Steps

1. Sample, Presenter: click slide 2 (Core Banking, live)
2. Rail > Milestones > v1
3. Capture on slide 2's card
4. Designer: Current (live), Milestone properties of v1 > Delete
5. Presenter: click slide 2

## What differs

```text
Expected: As Add slide on a milestone: the slide is linked to v1 with no copy, and after Delete milestone it shows the live model (78 nodes)
Actual: After Capture: snapshotId null, modelSnapshot of 11 nodes, no milestone tag on the card; after deleting v1 the slide still draws v1 (8 drawn, '11 nodes · 5 relations')
Seen: 3 of 3
Where it probably comes from: captureSlideViewport in packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts copies c4Nodes into modelSnapshot when the slide is unlinked, even with activeSnapshotId set, and does not link it as addPresentationSlide does
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests/presenterSlides.test.ts: selectMilestone(v1), captureSlideViewport on an unlinked slide, expect snapshotId === v1 and no modelSnapshot; after removeSnapshot(v1), previewSlide shows the live model
```

## Fixed

2026-10-22 (fix round of round 170–175, branch fix/r170): Capture on a milestone links the slide to it with no copy, so after Delete milestone the slide shows the live model.
