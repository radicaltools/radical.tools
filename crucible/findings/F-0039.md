## Steps

1. Sample, System Context, Designer
2. click Focus on the canvas controls (off)
3. Settings > Smart fit (off)
4. Settings > untick Wizard on create
5. reload

## What differs

```text
Expected: Focus off (aria-pressed false) and Smart fit off after the reload
Actual: the wizard toggle saves {nodeWizardOnCreate:false, smartFit:true, alignKeepsOrder:true, selectionFocus:true}; after the reload Focus aria-pressed=true and Settings shows 'Smart fit (on)'
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/studio/src/renderer/src/components/Toolbar.tsx RailMenus: studioSettings state read once at mount; setNodeWizardOnCreate saves {...prev} with stale smartFit/selectionFocus
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/selection-focus.spec.ts: Focus off, toggle Wizard on create, reload; Focus aria-pressed stays false (same for Smart fit)
```

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

Every writer of the studio settings now changes only its own setting over the ones stored now (updateStudioSettings), and the Settings menu follows changes made elsewhere.
