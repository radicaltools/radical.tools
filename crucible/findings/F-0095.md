## Steps

1. Bookstore fixture open in Designer, Smart fit off
2. Models > Manage models > Open… > Sample model
3. Read the camera of System Context, reload

## What differs

```text
Expected: System Context opens at its saved camera {64,14,0.55}, as when the sample is opened from the welcome screen
Actual: It opens at {68,80,0.4}, the sample's defaultViewport (All elements); that is kept in radical-view-cameras as view-ctx's, and after a reload the view opens at {68,80,0.4} again
Seen: 3 of 3
Where it probably comes from: guess: ModelSteps.openOnView's store subscription calls setActiveView inside loadDiagram's set(); loadDiagram then ends with __rfPlaceCamera(defaultVP) on the view now shown, and Canvas keep() stores it (the DSL import uses openOnView too)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/views.spec.ts: Smart fit off, a model open, open the sample from Models; the camera equals view-ctx's saved viewport, before and after a reload
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

loadDiagram places the camera of the canvas shown when it ends, so a model opened on a view from Models (or a Structurizr import) opens at that view's camera and keeps it after a reload.
