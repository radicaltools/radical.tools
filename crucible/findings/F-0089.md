## Steps

1. Sample, Designer, Core Banking: select Fraud Detection, Delete, Remove from model
2. Fold Access & Security; View properties of Core Banking, Lines: Orthogonal
3. Milestones: open v1, v2, v3, v4
4. Open v3, fold Risk & Data, Propagate; open v4

## What differs

```text
Expected: Every milestone draws Core Banking with Access & Security folded and orthogonal lines (as v1 and v2 do); after the Propagate from v3, v4 shows Risk & Data folded too
Actual: v1 and v2: folded, orthogonal; v3 and v4: open, 8 curved lines. After the Propagate: current, v1, v2, v3 show Risk & Data folded, v4 open. Saved view-core: current [[dom-risk],orthogonal], v3 copy [[dom-risk],model], v4 copy [null,model]
Seen: 3 of 3
Where it probably comes from: guess: showMilestoneViews shows the milestone's copy (milestoneOwnView, made by keepViewsInMilestones at the delete) with everything but name, folder, places and camera, so collapsedNodeIds, edgeStyle, layoutConstraints, hiddenRelationIds and the filter are frozen at the delete; commitMilestoneChanges applies view edits only to the live views and the edited milestone's copy, never to later milestones' copies
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests/milestoneDeletedSince.test.ts: delete an element v3 and v4 hold; toggle a collapse and setViewEdgeStyle on that view on the live model; selectMilestone(v4): collapsed and orthogonal; and Propagate of a collapse from v3 reaches v4
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

A milestone keeps of a view only what it shows differently (its list of elements deleted live since), so live folds and line style reach it, and Propagate reaches the later milestones' views (model.milestoneView).
