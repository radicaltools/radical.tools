## Steps

1. Sample model, Designer, Settings > Smart fit off, reload
2. System Context: wheel zoom in, drag KYC / AML Provider
3. open Core Banking, wheel zoom out
4. Ctrl+Z
5. open Core Banking again

## What differs

```text
Expected: Undo back on System Context with KYC back and the camera {-159,-160,0.834}; Core Banking again at {222,92,0.318}
Actual: System Context at {64,14,0.55} (the camera saved at load), Core Banking at {67,26,0.45}; control: Ctrl+Z on System Context itself keeps the camera
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts _restoreHistoryEntry: restores entry.views wholesale, viewport included (it keeps treemapFocusId/wikiFocusId/tableActiveTab 'like a camera' but not viewport), and does not save the outgoing view's camera before __rfPlaceCamera
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests (history/views): set view A's and B's viewports, make a step on A, switch to B with a new camera, undo: views[A].viewport and views[B].viewport are the latest ones, not the entry's; e2e variant in apps/e2e/tests/studio/views.spec.ts
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

_restoreHistoryEntry keeps every view's current camera and saves the camera of the view being left before Undo or Redo switches canvas.
