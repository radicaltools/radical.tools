## Steps

1. Bookstore fixture, Containers view
2. Hold Alt and drag a box from the empty inside of the Bookstore frame over its children, in Designer and then in Viewer

## What differs

```text
Expected: In Viewer and Presenter as in Designer: the box selects web, api and db
Actual: Designer selects [web, api, db]; Viewer selects [bookstore]; the document does not change in either
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: the box selection in Viewer/Presenter starts as a click on the frame (a drag of the system) instead of a box on its empty inside
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio: Alt box from inside a system frame selects the same children in Designer, Viewer and Presenter
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

In Viewer and Presenter a special-key drag from a frame starts the box at the press and selects what it covers; in Designer the same drag from inside a frame draws a line, by design (the run's Designer box had started just outside the frame).
