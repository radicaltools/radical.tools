## Steps

1. Open the bookstore fixture, Containers view, Designer
2. Drag the Bookstore system down (it is pinned)
3. Click it to select it
4. Click the Unpin pin at its top-right corner

## What differs

```text
Expected: The pin comes off; the container stays expanded where it is
Actual: Bookstore collapses (860x350 -> 280x180, 3 children no longer drawn) and stays pinned; elementsFromPoint at the pin's centre is the Unpin button. Enter on the focused pin unpins; a click on a leaf's pin unpins
Seen: 3 of 3
Where it probably comes from: guess: the pin overlay (packages/ui/src/components/AlignmentGuides.tsx) sits on the collapse button's enlarged hit area (.c4-node-collapse-btn::before inset -10px, packages/ui/src/index.css)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio: drag a container, select it, mouse-click getByTestId('unpin-node'): no pin in the view's layoutConstraints and the children still drawn
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

Canvas' hand-over of a click to a nearby −/+ skips clicks on a control of its own, so the Unpin pin unpins and the container stays expanded.
