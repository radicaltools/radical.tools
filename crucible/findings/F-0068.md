## Steps

1. Bookstore moved to a folder
2. Right-click Customer → Discuss, type a draft
3. Press F5 (or Cmd+P)

## What differs

```text
Expected: As in the Quick search field and the Models dialog: F5 prevented=true (no reload), Cmd+P prevented in a dialog
Actual: In the discussion comment field: F5 prevented=false, Ctrl+P prevented=false, Meta+P prevented=false (Models dialog: F5 true, Meta+P true; Quick search: F5 true)
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/studio/src/renderer/src/components/DiscussionPanel.tsx: the aside's onKeyDown stopPropagation() keeps Toolbar's F5 and QuickSearch's Cmd+P window listeners (which preventDefault, then defer to keysTakenByDialog) from seeing the key
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/web-discussions.spec.ts: with the comment field focused, dispatch F5 and Mod+P and assert defaultPrevented is true and the draft survives
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

keepKeysHere lets F5 and Cmd/Ctrl+P go on to Studio from the discussion panel, the Discuss menu, the Warnier popover and step name, and the milestone name fields.
