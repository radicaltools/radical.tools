## Steps

1. Sample, Designer, Smart fit off, System Context: wheel zoom in
2. Presenter: click slide '1 – System Context'
3. Reload in Presenter
4. Click Designer

## What differs

```text
Expected: Designer's System Context at {-107,-160,0.834}, as without the reload (control in the script)
Actual: {64,14,0.55}, the slide's camera; radical-view-cameras' view-ctx becomes the slide's camera on the slide click (Viewer's camera moves are kept the same way)
Seen: 3 of 3
Where it probably comes from: guess: packages/ui/src/components/Canvas.tsx keep subscription skips only presentationActive, not appMode presenter/viewer
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/presentation.spec.ts: Designer camera C; Presenter, click a slide of that view; reload; Designer: the camera is C
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

Only Designer's camera is kept as a view's camera; Viewer and Presenter keep none and open each view at Designer's, so a slide clicked in Presenter no longer changes the camera Designer opens the view at.
