## Steps

1. Sample model, Designer, Settings > Smart fit off, reload
2. System Context: wheel zoom in (or click Core Banking)
3. Reload

## What differs

```text
Expected: After the reload the view shows the camera it had: {-107,-160,0.834} after the zoom; kept outside the model (per-viewer state, e.g. browser storage or .radical/), so the model files do not change
Actual: The stored camera stays {64,14,0.55} after the zoom and after the click's framing, and the reload opens there
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: the view's camera is saved only with an edit (requirement 'No write-back of reloads'); nothing keeps a camera-only change
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/views.spec.ts: Smart fit off, wheel zoom on System Context, reload: the camera is the one before the reload, and the model's saved document is unchanged
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

Each view's camera is kept per viewer in browser storage (viewCameras.ts) and put back on load, so a camera-only move survives a reload without changing the model.
