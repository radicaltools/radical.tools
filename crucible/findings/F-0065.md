## Steps

1. Sample, Designer, Payment Flow
2. Milestones: + New milestone 'M'
3. Sequences: Payment Processing, Delete sequence
4. Open M on Payment Flow

## What differs

```text
Expected: M holds seq-payment-flow with 7 steps, so Payment Flow plays them in M
Actual: Payment Flow in M shows 'No sequence linked. This view is set to Flow…'; the live view's sequenceId is undefined and M keeps no view of its own (M.views['view-payflow'] absent)
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: guess: packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts removeSequence unlinks sequenceId on every view; on the current model it should leave milestones holding the sequence their own copy of the view (model.milestoneOwnView), as the F-0053 fix does for deleted elements
A test that would catch it: packages/ui/tests (beside milestoneDeletedSince.test.ts): removeSequence on the current model, selectMilestone of one holding it: the flow view's sequenceId is the sequence's in the milestone, undefined on the current model
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

Deleting a sequence on the live model, or propagating its delete, leaves milestones that hold it their own flow views linked to it (keepSequenceViewsInMilestones), in Studio and over MCP.
