## Steps

1. Open > Sample model, view Core Banking
2. Milestones > v3
3. Models > Import from Hub > Secrets > Add to Model > Add to model
4. Continue editing (decide later)
5. Discard

## What differs

```text
Expected: Live model as before: 78 elements, 17 views
Actual: The live saved document goes from 17 to 18 views before the choice is made; after Discard: 78 elements, 18 views; view 'Secrets' in Concerns lists 23 elements, 22 of them not in the model; its card says '1 node'
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: apps/studio/src/renderer/src/hub/importConcept.ts writes state.views directly; milestone Discard (liveBackup) does not take back views added by the import (guess; sibling of IMPROVEMENTS item 116)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/milestones.spec.ts: import a pack in v3, Discard, stored views equal those before and no view lists a missing element
```

## Fixed

2026-10-22 (fix round of round 170–175, branch fix/r170): A pack imported in a milestone brings its view only when its elements reach the live model, so Discard leaves the live views as they were.
