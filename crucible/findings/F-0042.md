## Steps

1. Seed a wiki with the Specification chain (needs → requirements → scenarios, mockups), need 'Pay by card' and requirement 'Tell the customer why' deriving from it
2. Open the requirement's page: the line reads 'Add: Requirements'; Add or link… offers only a scenario or a mockup
3. Write 'Log every failed payment' in the line

## What differs

```text
Expected: The page offers Scenarios and Mockups (the chain's next level, as Add or link… and the table's + row do), and what is written there shows on it
Actual: Offers only Requirements; the requirement written gets derives → the requirement, is shown 0 times on the page, and a warning says the view's filter does not show it; no line for Scenarios or Mockups (a need's page likewise offers Needs)
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/components/WikiView.tsx WikiChildren: writable under an element uses childTypesOf (hierarchyRelation/containment) and writeElements links via hierarchyRelation, not addChoices(view, ctx, {node, depth}) (a guess)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/wiki.spec.ts: Specification wiki with a need and a derived requirement; on the requirement's page .wiki-write-start offers Scenarios and Mockups, not Requirements; a scenario written there gets verifies → the requirement and shows on the page
```

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

Under an element the wiki's writing line offers what the view's chain shows at the next level (a requirement's page: Scenarios and Mockups), and links what is written the chain's way, as Add or link… does.
