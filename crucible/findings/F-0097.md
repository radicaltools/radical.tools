## Steps

1. Sample, Designer, Smart fit off, System Context: wheel zoom in
2. F5, then → to slide 2 (Core Banking)
3. Esc
4. Reload

## What differs

```text
Expected: After Esc System Context at {-107,-160,0.834}, the camera it had before F5
Actual: System Context drawn at {67,26,0.45}, slide 2's camera (Core Banking), with elements under the palette; a reload then jumps to {-107,-160,0.834}. The same after a reload while playing
Seen: 4 of 4
Where it probably comes from: guess: diagramStore.stopPresentation puts back nodes, views, activeViewId and selection from __prePresState but never the camera; startPresentation does not keep it
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/presentation.spec.ts: Smart fit off, set a camera, F5, →, Esc: the camera equals the one before F5
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

startPresentation keeps the camera with the rest of Designer's state and stopPresentation puts it back, so Esc after F5 returns to the camera from before F5 and a reload keeps it.
