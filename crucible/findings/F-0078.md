## What differs

```text
Expected: the three relations listed one per line, technology per relation or none
Actual: textContent 'Admin API calls\nAPI calls\nAPI calls' drawn with white-space normal as one run-on name; technology '[HTTPS / Admin API]' of the first only though two are HTTPS / REST; the same when hovered
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/store/diagramStore.ts deriveRFEdges joins labels with \n and keeps the first technology; packages/ui/src/index.css .relation-label-name keeps no line breaks (guess)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio: sample System Context, the rolled-up label's innerText has three lines and does not show one relation's technology alone
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

A rolled-up line draws each relation's name on its own line and shows a technology only when all its relations share it, on the canvas and in the layouts' input and sizing.
