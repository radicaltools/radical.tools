## Steps

1. Open the sample, view Authorization (curved or orthogonal)
2. Zoom onto the need 'Everyone sees only theirs'

## What differs

```text
Expected: a line enters its end element from the side it ends at, never crossing the element itself
Actual: both derives lines run 78 px (orthogonal 75 px) inside the 200x80 card before coming back into its left side; Smart Layout keeps them (2 -> 2) and adds one on Data consistency (0 -> 1); at 31dcffed both came straight in from the left (0 such lines)
Seen: 3 of 3 curved, 1 of 1 orthogonal; 0 of 1 on the 31dcffed build
Where it probably comes from: packages/layout/src/edgeRouting.ts computeRoutedEdge/routeAround: the ends are no obstacles and only keepOut (60 deep x 80 wide) guards them, blocked unpadded since F-0076, so A* passes through the rest of the end element (guess)
A test that would catch it: packages/layout/tests/edgeRouting.test.ts: computeRoutedEdge into the left side of a 200x80 box from sources above-left and below-left, with obstacles that force a detour: no point of the route lies inside the end box beyond the port; apps/e2e: sample Authorization, no drawn path enters its own end element
```

## Fixed (2026-10-26)

A routed line keeps out of the boxes of the elements it joins (only the grid points inside them are blocked, so exits stay open on every grid origin) and enters them only at its ports; lines through their own ends on the sample's canvases went from 5 to 0 curved and 4 to 0 orthogonal.
