## Steps

1. Open the sample in Presenter (not playing)
2. Slide 1's card: Link milestone → v2
3. Milestones tab: pick v1
4. Click slide 2 (unlinked), or slide 1 (v2)

## What differs

```text
Expected: The chip names the model drawn: none or live for the unlinked slide, v2 for slide 1
Actual: Unlinked slide 2 draws the live 14 elements (v1: 8) under 'Milestone: v1 – Core & Accounts ⊙ diff', URL /s/snap-v1; slide 1 draws v2's 4 (v1: 3), search says 17 nodes, chip and Milestones list say v1. With nothing picked, the v2 slide shows v2 with no chip
Seen: 2 of 2
Where it probably comes from: guess: selecting a slide in the dock applies its model but leaves activeSnapshotId (the chip, the URL) at the picked milestone; F-0027's fix covers only a playing slide
```
