## Steps

1. Bookstore fixture, Containers view
2. Click Web App, Alt+click API
3. Alt-drag Web App onto Orders DB: 2 relations made
4. Ctrl+Z once

## What differs

```text
Expected: One Ctrl+Z gives back the document before the gesture: 4 relations; Auth Service in Access & Security and Fraud Detection in Risk & Data
Actual: After one Ctrl+Z: 5 relations (4 → 6 → 5); nest: Fraud Detection back in Risk & Data, Auth Service still in Banking Services; a second Ctrl+Z is needed. The nest shows two notices 'Moved 1 node into Group.'
Seen: 3 of 3 (relate), 2 of 2 (nest)
Where it probably comes from: packages/ui/src/components/Canvas.tsx, connect mouseup: addRelation per source and nestAll → reparentNodes per old parent, each _pushUndo, not wrapped in _asOneStep (read in the code)
A test that would catch it: apps/e2e/tests/studio/nesting.spec.ts: after relating/nesting several selected elements at once (nest from two parents), one Control+Z restores the saved relations and parents
```

## Fixed (2026-10-23)

A gesture on several selected elements, relating or nesting them (also from different parents), is one undo step with one notice.
